## §0 — Read-First Summary & Checklist (staff-facing)

**What this playbook is.** REF-02 says *what every lesson plan must contain* (the 8-field Spine, the Lesson Flow, the Flex Zone). REF-03 says *what those fields mean for Bangla specifically* — which content is mandatory, which words must appear, which Bloom mix fits each class, which examples and discovery patterns are pre-approved, when each topic is revised, and where each year's emphasis goes.

**What it is NOT.** It is not a lesson plan, not a curriculum, and not a re-print of REF-19. It is the subject rulebook a Bangla lesson-plan author reads *before* writing a single lesson.

**The one Bangla principle.** *Decoding is a ladder; meaning is the goal; free thinking and Islamic alignment are protected at every rung.* The decoding ladder (বর্ণ → কারচিহ্ন → ফলা → রেফ → যুক্তবর্ণ) is the High-load-bearing spine — get it solid in the early years. On top of it sit the four PROTECTED competencies that the structural metric under-weights but the school's mission elevates.

**The author's 8-step checklist (run before writing any Bangla lesson plan — *or, in REF-02 v1.3, a Chapter Plan + its Session Plans*):**

- [ ] **0.** *(v1.1, D-050)* **Decide the production stage**: this chapter is either (a) **single-period** → write one standalone **Session Plan** that inlines the Spine; or (b) **multi-period** → write the **Chapter Plan** first (Spine + §2.9 Session Map), then one **Session Plan** per period referencing it (REF-02 §2A). Session count = the chapter's TG-Reconciliation period count, within REF-02 §2A.3's ±40% / ±1 band; set **once**, at chapter-planning, before any Session Plan is written. Steps 1–8 below run **at the Chapter Plan stage**; each Session Plan thereafter inherits its slice from the Chapter Plan's §2.9 Session Map and adds only Layers 2–3.
- [ ] **1.** Find the পাঠ in the relevant class's REF-05 skeleton; pull its TG-validated যোগ্যতা/শিখনফল from the matching `completed_C{n}_BAN_TGReconciliation_v1`.

REF-02's Spine has eight numbered fields. This section gives the Bangla form of each. Fields REF-02 already fully specifies (§2.4 mechanics, §2.7/§2.8 homework structure) carry only Bangla notes; the fields REF-02 explicitly defers to the Subject Spine Playbook (§2.2, §2.3) carry the substantive specialization.

### 2.1 Learning Objective — Bangla verbs + the NCF যোগ্যতা framework

REF-02 §2.1 requires one observable verb matching the §2.3 Bloom level. For Bangla:

- **Observable Bangla verb modes** (pick the mode the objective is checkable in): oral (বলবে, পড়বে, উচ্চারণ করবে, আবৃত্তি করবে, শোনাবে), written (লিখবে, ভেঙে লিখবে, বানাবে, পূরণ করবে), graphic (চিহ্নিত করবে, মেলাবে, দেখাবে), produced artifact (নিজের মতো করে লিখবে / বলবে — the free-thinking mode).

### 2.2 Must-Cover Content — the decoding ladder + vocabulary

This is the Bangla form of REF-02 §2.2 (D-021). Bangla Must-Cover Content has two parts: **(A) the decoding-ladder rung** the lesson sits on, and **(B) the required vocabulary**.

**(A) The decoding ladder (the High-load-bearing spine).** Taught strictly in order; no rung pre-taught before the one below it is solid (REF-19 load-bearing + the C1→C2 critical handoff):

| Rung | Element | Where formally taught | Load-bearing (REF-19) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **বর্ণ (alphabet)** — 11 vowels + 39 consonants + special signs (ং ঃ ঁ) | C1 (full acquisition, পাঠ 10–39); C2 পাঠ 5 re-anchor only | 🔴 High |
| 2 | **কারচিহ্ন (vowel signs)** | C1 (concept পাঠ 18, then interleaved 21–31); C2 পাঠ 7 consolidate; C3 পাঠ 4 review | 🔴 High |
| 3 | **ফলাচিহ্ন (consonant-signs)** | C2 পাঠ 12 (first taught); C3 পাঠ 5 (formally re-taught) | 🟠 Medium |
| 4 | **রেফ** | C2 পাঠ 13 | 🟢 Low (maintenance after) |
| 5 | **যুক্তবর্ণ (conjuncts)** | C1 *selected, as whole sight-units*; C2 পাঠ 10 *systematic construction*; C3 পাঠ 5 *formal competency*; C4 পাঠ 4 *disambiguation* | 🔴 High |

> **Rule B-1 — the conjunct rule** *(TG §5.B — C1 softening; C3 promotion).*
> **C1:** *Selected* যুক্তবর্ণ **do appear at Class 1 as whole sight-units** — heard, said, read, and written per NCF যোগ্যতা ১.১ / ৫.১ / ৯.১ / ১৩.২. There is **no *systematic* conjunct construction at C1** (that is C2's standalone lesson, পাঠ 10, "ভেঙে লিখি" ১৩.১.৩). **Do not over-defer:** a C1 lesson that needs a selected conjunct as a whole unit uses it; it simply does not break conjuncts down systematically.
> **C2:** the systematic construction lesson (পাঠ 10).
> **C3:** conjuncts **and** fola are a **formal hear/say/read/write competency** (পাঠ 5, যোগ্যতা ১.১/৫.১/৫.২/৯.১/১৩.১) — *not* "embedded review."
> **C4:** disambiguation of confusable conjuncts (পাঠ 4, ত/ত্র, ক/ক্ষ).
> *(This rule supersedes the C1 skeleton §6.4 line "যুক্তবর্ণ পরিহার করুন … গৌণ," which predates the C1 TG reconciliation.)*

**Mechanics review-vs-teach handoff** *(TG §5.B — C2 mechanics note).* The TG marks some early-class mechanics lessons as **explicit no-যোগ্যতা reviews** (C2 পাঠ 5 alphabet, পাঠ 7 kar; C3 পাঠ 4 matra carry an explicit "এ পাঠের জন্য সুনির্দিষ্ট কোনো অর্জন-উপযোগী যোগ্যতা নেই" box) versus **full-যোগ্যতা teaches** (C2 পাঠ 12 fola, পাঠ 13 রেফ; C3 পাঠ 5 fola/conjunct). An author must set the objective accordingly: a review lesson's objective is consolidation/recall, not first-teaching.

**(B) Required vocabulary** (REF-01 §4.2/§4.3 — word-level, position-free):
- **C1:** the consolidated **99-word "core"** (present in all 10 years — লিখি, বলি, আমাদের, পাঠ, নাম, কী, গাছ, করি, ছবি, শব্দ, পড়ি, শুনি, আছে, ফুল, শিখি, দেখি, কার, পানি, একটি …) **+ the 188 Era-3 stable words.** This is a **school asset** — keep teaching from it even though NCF distributes vocabulary per-lesson (REF-19 `BAN-VOCAB` delivery-shift note). Replacement content must reuse ≥10–15 core words per story (C1 skeleton §6.1).
- **C2–C5:** per-পাঠ themed vocabulary (NCF distributed/per-lesson glossary; C3 keeps a consolidated end-glossary — a presentation choice, not a framework marker). The required-word list for any lesson is its TG থিম vocabulary plus any earlier-tier core word the lesson reuses.

**Rules (Bangla-specific, in addition to REF-02 §2.2 rules):**
1. The decoding-ladder order is non-negotiable; a lesson may not assume a higher rung than its class has taught.
2. C1 stories stay 60–120 words; ছড়া 4–8 lines; short S-O-V sentences (C1 skeleton §6.1).
3. F-01 holds on every word and example (no Western names/foods/clothing).

### 2.3 Bloom's-Level Distribution — Bangla per class band

- **Class 1–2 band** (from REF-02 §2.3 / REF-06 §3.6): Remember 35–45%, Understand 30–40%, Apply 15–25%, Analyze 0–10%, Evaluate 0–5%, Create 0–5%. First-introduction letter/sound lessons may be Remember-dominant with justification; the **own-way/free-writing lessons reach into Create even at C1–2** and are explicitly permitted there (they are the PROTECTED strand — §3.4).
- **Class 3–5 bands:** use **REF-06 §3.6** for the exact indicative shares (the recall share falls and Analyze/Evaluate/Create rise as the class rises). Not re-printed here to avoid drift; read them from REF-06 when C3–5 production begins.

> **Bangla Bloom rule — the C4 story split** *(TG §5.B — C4 STORY).* "Story" is not one Bloom emphasis at C4. The TG uses two bundles:
> - **Folktale / animal stories** (C4 পাঠ 8 চিল ও বিড়াল, পাঠ 11 বাঘখেকো শিয়ালের ছানা) → the **literary-recitation cluster** (৪.১/৮.১/১২.১ — enjoy, recite, retell "নিজের মতো করে"). Bloom emphasis: Understand + Apply + a Create reach (own-words retell).
> - **Prose narratives** (C4 পাঠ 16 সোনার থালা, পাঠ 19 লাল জামা) → the **descriptive/comprehension cluster** (৩.১/৭.১/১১.১/১৫.১ — analyse content, explain, write explanation). Bloom emphasis: Understand + Analyze.
> The author sets the §2.3 mix from which bundle the story sits in — not from the word "story."

### 2.4 Exit-Check — Bangla forms

Follow REF-02 §2.4 (one observable check, 2–5 min, per-student readout, same Bloom level as §2.1). **Per-student readout ≠ one question per student.** A 12-pupil class does **not** get 12 different questions — it gets **12 readouts of the *same* check** (each child points / says / writes / shows while the teacher circulates and observes each one). The check is identical for everyone so the readouts stay comparable across pupils and across classes (REF-02 §2.4 rule 3). A small set of **2–4 variants** is used only to deter copying in a *written* check — never one-question-per-child. The author **picks the lesson's exit-check from the Bangla Exit-Check Bank (§3.6)** so a teacher who is stuck always has a ready form. The 30% systemic / 3-lesson individual thresholds (D-025) apply unchanged.

## §3 — The Bangla Flex Zone & pedagogical posture (specializing REF-02 §4)

### 3.1 What a Bangla teacher MAY / MAY NOT decide
Inherits REF-02 §4.1/§4.2 unchanged. Bangla-specific points:
- **MAY:** choose which §3.2 Example-Bank word illustrates a letter/conjunct; choose a §3.3 discovery pattern; reorder Guided/Independent practice; add plain clapping syllable-rhythm (literacy device, **not** music — C-03).
- **MAY NOT:** skip any decoding-ladder rung in §2.2(A); pre-teach a higher rung than the class has reached (**rule B-1**); replace an oral exit-check with written-only where the objective is pronunciation; teach a NEEDS-REPLACEMENT element as KEEP-AS-IS; (new/probationary teachers) reverse Hook ↔ Direct-Instruction.

### 3.2 Bangla Example Bank conventions (REF-02 §4.4)
**Form for Bangla = words / short phrases**, minimum 3 per teaching point.
- Examples are concrete, age-banded, and drawn from the §2.2(B) vocabulary tiers where possible (so examples double as required-word practice).
- All examples F-01-clean and curation-clean; Islamic-resonant words are welcome `[Islamic-add]` (e.g. for আ: আম, আকাশ, **আল্লাহ**; for ই: ইট, ইঁদুর, **ইমান**; for ঈ: ঈগল, **ঈদ**, **ঈমানদার**).
- For living-being example words, use the **word only**, not a picture (C-05).
- The author lists the bank per teaching point in the lesson plan; the teacher picks or substitutes (substitution logged for the Subject Lead).

### 3.3 Bangla Discovery-Pattern library (REF-02 §4.5)
Minimum 3 per lesson; concrete enough for a new teacher to run unaided. The canonical Bangla patterns:

1. **Flashcard-led** — show prior letters/conjuncts; students name; transition to new (Hook; decoding lessons).
2. **Object-led** — bring a real object whose name carries the target letter (আম for আ, ইট for ই); students name → letter. *(No animal depiction — word only.)*
3. **Sorting-led** — mixed letter/conjunct cards; students sort "known / new" or "single letter / conjunct."
4. **Story-led** — tell a short story; students predict the next event or name the lesson/moral (গল্প, কবিতা, biography, INFOTEXT).
5. **Question-led** — open "কেন / কী হলে কী হতো" question before content; students hypothesise (comprehension, free-thinking).
6. **Paired-exploration** — students discuss in pairs (জোড়ায় কাজ) before whole-class share (the NCF collaborative manner; oracy, comprehension).
7. **Picture-to-sentence (ছবি দেখে বাক্য)** — students build sentences from a scene (GUIDEDWRITE → SENTENCE).
8. **Own-way prompt (নিজের মতো করে লিখি / বলি)** — open prompt; each student answers in their own words (the free-thinking pattern; FREEWRITE).
9. **Role-reading / dialogue-reading (ভূমিকায় পড়ি)** — for the C4/C5 নাটিকা lessons (অমল ও দইওয়ালা; **কুপোকাত**): students read assigned lines aloud **as themselves**, not impersonating a character (C-19 boundary). *(TG §5.B — C5 drama: the playbook carries a drama pattern because নাটিকা survives at C5 — see §5.)*
10. **Learner-question / title-prediction (পাঠ থেকে প্রশ্ন বানাই / পাঠের শিরোনাম কী হতে পারে)** — students generate their own questions or predict a title (the self-discovery pattern; LEARNERQ — NCF names it at C4, may run lightly/orally earlier as Flex enrichment).

### 3.4 Free thinking within an Islamic framing — the PROTECTED competencies

*(This section operationalizes Instructions §6 for Bangla and folds in the free-thinking findings from all five TG reconciliations — TG §5.B, every class.)*

REF-19's load-bearing metric scores these four topics **Low on dependency**, which would wrongly read as "safe to compress." REF-19 §4A tags them **🟦 PROTECTED**; this playbook treats them as **never the first to cut when time is short**:

| PROTECTED competency | Why protected | Where it lives across the classes |
|---|---|---|
| **Free / own-way writing (মুক্ত রচনা, নিজের মতো করে লিখি)** | free-thinking flagship (Instructions §6) | **Originates at C1** (যোগ্যতা ১৬.২; method "একাকী চিন্তা"; পাঠ 35/41/50/51/53) — *not* merely a C2 start; **pervasive at C2 (পাঠ 24), C3 (~a dozen পাঠ, peak পাঠ 28), C4 (own-way ১৬.২ + retell ৪.১.৪/১১.১.১/১২.১.৩), C5 (own-opinion ১৫.২ across ~10 lessons).** |
| **Learner-generated questions (প্রশ্ন তৈরি)** | self-discovery flagship | NCF names it at **C4 (পাঠ 1)** + the title-prediction sibling (পাঠ 19). **Deeper-not-faster for the free-thinking strand:** when a class is ahead, enrich with richer prompts and more peer sharing — **do not** formalise free writing into exam essays early, and do not accelerate into the next class's Spine (REF-19 §1.4).

**Origin note (TG-confirmed):** the free-thinking strand is not a school graft onto a recall-only curriculum — it is **native to NCF** at every class. Emphasising it aligns the school's mission *with* NCF intent rather than against it.

### 3.5 Redirection patterns (the discovery-stays-Islamic move)
REF-02 §4.3 defers redirection patterns to REF-03. When a student's hypothesis, example, or own-way answer diverges from Islamic values, the teacher redirects **without shaming**:
- *Student offers a musical jingle / tune as a memory aid* → "সুন্দর! এবার সুর ছাড়া, ছন্দে পড়ে দেখি" — keep the rhythm, drop the melody (C-03).
- *Student names a Western pop figure / character as an example* → "ভালো চেষ্টা! আমাদের চেনা কারো নাম বলি তো" — redirect to a local/Muslim name (C-10/F-01).
- *Student's own-way story drifts into a festival/idolatry/veneration frame* → acknowledge the effort, redirect the setting to a permissible one (Eid, family, nature as Allah's signs) while keeping the child's idea (C-01/C-04/C-18).
- *Student personifies the sun/rain as the giver of life* → "কে দিয়েছেন সূর্যের আলো?" — re-anchor causation in Allah (C-09).
The redirection preserves the child's initiative (the free-thinking value) and corrects only the framing. These patterns also seed Project 07 teacher training.

## §5 — Per-class emphasis digest (consuming REF-19 v1.9)

This is a **digest**, not a re-print — REF-19 v1.9 remains the source of truth for the 22-topic map, the load-bearing tiers, and the §4A PROTECTED tags. For each class it answers the lesson-author's two questions: *what must be mastered this year (load-bearing)?* and *what must be protected this year regardless of structural weight (PROTECTED)?* — under the "deeper-not-faster" guardrail (REF-19 §1.4: ahead-of-pace classes go deeper in the same topic; never accelerate into the next class's Spine).

**Cross-class vertical anchors (emphasise the through-line, not just the year):**
- **The decoding ladder** (বর্ণ→কার→ফলা→রেফ→যুক্তবর্ণ) is the High-load-bearing backbone; its early rungs (C1 alphabet/matra, C2 systematic conjunct) are the highest-value mastery checkpoints in the whole subject.
- **Form-fill ১৪.৩** is a clean **C1 → C4 vertical anchor** (C1 পাঠ 54 আমার ঠিকানা/ছক পূরণ → C4 পাঠ 23) — treat C1's form-fill as the foundation of the C4 functional ladder, not a throwaway.
- **Free / own-way writing** runs **C1 → C5** continuously (১৬.২ → ১৫.২); it is the school's flagship free-thinking competency and is PROTECTED at every class.
- **Islamic Universal-Core anchors** run **C2 → C5** (Khandaq narrative → আবু বকর/উমর biographies → বিদায় হজ); PROTECTED on Islamic-alignment grounds.

### 5.1 — Class 1 emphasis
**Master (High):** alphabet, vowel signs, selected conjuncts (as whole units — rule B-1), word formation, the 99-word vocabulary core, small-sentence reading, listening–speaking. These are the foundation the entire stack rests on — mastery is non-negotiable. **Protect (🟦):** listening–speaking (collaborative manner) and **free/own-way writing — which *originates* at C1** (যোগ্যতা ১৬.২; "একাকী চিন্তা"; পাঠ 35/41/50/51/53), not at C2. **Deeper-not-faster:** an advanced C1 class enriches *within* C1 — more word-building, cleaner handwriting, oral blending, richer own-way prompts — and **must not pre-teach the systematic conjunct construction** (that is C2, rule B-1).
