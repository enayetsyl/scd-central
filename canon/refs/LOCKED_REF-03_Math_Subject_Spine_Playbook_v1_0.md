## §0 — Read-First Summary & Checklist (staff-facing)

**What this playbook is.** REF-02 says *what every lesson plan must contain* (the 8-field Spine, the Lesson Flow, the Flex Zone). REF-03-Math says *what those fields mean for Math specifically* — which content is mandatory, in what prerequisite order, which numbers/symbols/terms must appear, which Bloom mix fits each class, which worked examples and discovery patterns are pre-approved, when each topic is revised, and where each year's emphasis goes.

**What it is NOT.** It is not a lesson plan, not a curriculum, and not a re-print of REF-19. It is the subject rulebook a Math lesson-plan author reads *before* writing a single lesson.

**The one Math principle.** *Number sense is the foundation; the operations ladder carries it; reasoning and pattern (free thinking) are protected at every rung; and — like English and unlike Bangla — Islamic alignment in Math is built almost entirely through **replacement (Project 05) and curation (Project 02)**, not through protected source content. Math content is values-neutral.* Math's NCF framework is a **tight sequential dependency ladder** (count → place value → operations → fractions/factors → decimals → percentage), so getting each rung solid before the next is the whole game.

**The four structural facts every Math author must hold:**

1. **Math is the most vertically-dependent subject in the school.** REF-19's load-bearing scoring is **8 High / 8 Medium / 8 Low** across 24 topics — more top-heavy than Bangla (6/9/7) or English (5/11/7). This is real, not an artefact: more topics sit on a high-load spine because numeracy is a chain, not a web. **The prerequisite order is non-negotiable (rule M-1).** A lesson may not assume a rung the class has not yet taught — the sharpest case being **place value before carrying** (C1 → C2 handoff).
2. **No Islamic-Universal-Core PROTECTED anchor exists in Math** (the same shape as English, unlike Bangla's three protected Islamic texts). Both 🟦 PROTECTED Math topics — `MATH-SENTENCE` and `MATH-PATTERN` — qualify on **free-thinking** grounds only. Math is the **lowest-risk subject for Islamic alignment**: its touchpoints are illustration/context **NEEDS-REPLACEMENT negatives** (counting pictures of living beings, currency-note portraits, the Shaheed-Minar floral intro, a cockfighting/fancy-dress pictograph, mixed-gender figures) routed to Project 02 / Project 05, plus one Islamic-**favourable** fact (the C5 শতকরা chapter teaches profit/loss with **no interest / সুদ**). **Implication: Math's alignment workload is almost entirely replacement + curation, lighter even than English on protect-in-place.**
3. **Chapter order ≠ strand order (rule M-2).** A chapter's NCF যোগ্যতা strand is read from **its own header**, never inferred from the chapter number. This caught real C4 mis-tags (Ch6 গুণিতক ও গুণনীয়ক is strand **২.৮**, not "৬.x"; corrected in `completed_C4_MATH_Skeleton_v2.md` via the D-042 TG check). The Math analogue of the English "TG-is-source-of-truth" lesson.
4. **Two DROP/NCF events + the LCM/GCD deferral are sequencing changes, not gaps (rule M-3).** Under NCF: **division and fractions are dropped from Class 2 and begin fresh at Class 3**; **LCM/GCD moves from Era-A Class 4 to Class 5**. None is a prerequisite gap — the NCF entry point simply moved later. Brief teachers; bridge with a Flex-Zone supplement only if the school values the earlier placement (never a Spine deletion).

### 2.2 Must-Cover Content — the operations dependency ladder + math vocabulary

This is the Math form of REF-02 §2.2 (D-021). Math Must-Cover Content has two parts: **(A) the dependency-ladder position** the lesson sits on, and **(B) the required numbers, symbols, and terms.**

**(A) The dependency ladder (the High-load-bearing spine).** Taught strictly in prerequisite order; no rung pre-taught before the one below it is solid (REF-19 load-bearing + prerequisite edges). The 🔴 High-load topics and their prerequisites:

| Topic (REF-19) | Builds on (prerequisite) | First formally taught | Why High-load |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Counting & number sense** `MATH-COUNT` | `MATH-COMPARE` | C1 (to 100) → rises each year (1000 → lakh → crore) | underpins all operations, money, measurement |
| **Place value** `MATH-PLACEVAL` | `MATH-COUNT` | **C1 U10 (১.৪ — NCF-new 🆕)** → C2 elevated → lakh/crore | gates carrying/borrowing, multi-digit ×/÷, decimals, money |
| **Addition** `MATH-ADD` | `MATH-COUNT`, `MATH-PLACEVAL` | C1 (২.১) → C2 carry | feeds subtraction-as-inverse, ×, four-op, money, measurement |
| **Subtraction** `MATH-SUB` | `MATH-COUNT`, `MATH-PLACEVAL`, `MATH-ADD` | C1 (২.২) → C2 borrow | add↔sub relationship, four-op, money |
| **Multiplication** `MATH-MUL` | `MATH-ADD` (repeated), `MATH-COUNT` | **C2 (২.৪) — starts C2, not C1** | division-as-inverse, four-op, factors, average, percentage, area |
| **Division** `MATH-DIV` | `MATH-MUL`, `MATH-SUB` | **C3 (২.৫) — begins C3 under NCF** | mul↔div relationship, four-op, fractions, average, LCM/GCD |
| **Measurement** `MATH-MEASURE` | `MATH-COUNT`, `MATH-MUL` (area), `MATH-DECIMAL` (C5) | C2 (৭.১) → C4 perimeter & area | area feeds geometry; quantities feed money/data |
| **Geometry** `MATH-GEOMETRY` | `MATH-MEASURE` (area facets) | C1 (৬.১) → C5 3-D solids 🆕 | a steady five-class spiral; feeds area/measurement |

> **Rule M-1 — the prerequisite-order rule.** The ladder order above is non-negotiable. A lesson may not assume a rung the class has not yet taught. The two sharpest handoffs: **(i)** confirm C1 **place-value** mastery before the C2 **carrying** step (the closed PREREQ-GAP — keep it closed); **(ii)** confirm **multiplication** fluency (C2/C3 tables) before C3 **division** and before C4 multi-digit work.

> **Rule M-3 — the NCF-resequencing rule** (do not read it as a gap). **Division** and **fractions** are **not taught at NCF Class 2** (Era-2 taught them; NCF dropped both) and **begin fresh at Class 3** (division ২.৫, fractions ৩.১). **LCM/GCD** is an Era-A Class-4 topic that under NCF **lands at Class 5** (২.৮); C4's ২.৮ covers primes/composites/divisibility **without** LCM/GCD. None is a prerequisite gap. If the school values the earlier placement, a **Flex-Zone supplement** bridges it — never a Spine deletion.

**The CPA sequence (concrete → pictorial → abstract).** Every new numeracy concept is introduced with a **concrete** model (real objects, counters, the "birds flying away" model for subtraction, sharing for division), then a **pictorial** model (number line, place-value blocks, fraction bars), then the **abstract** symbol/algorithm. An author who jumps to the algorithm skips the rung that makes it stick. **CPA must be written visibly into the lesson plan, not held only in the author's head:** each Must-Cover teaching point and each §3.2 Example-Bank entry is **labelled (C) / (P) / (A)**, and the Lesson Flow **names the CPA stage** each step is at. Most of our teachers are not strongly trained (Strategic Context problem 1–2), so the explicit C/P/A labels are how the teacher *learns and applies* the sequence while delivering — the label is a teaching aid for the teacher, not just a planning note for the author.

**(B) Required numbers, symbols & terms** (REF-01 §4.2/§4.3 — the Math three-way split per the Stability Playbook §1 Q5: *number sequence + operation symbols + math vocabulary terms*):
- **Number sequence (the year's ceiling):** C1 to 100 · C2 to 1000 · C3 to lakh · C4 to crore (নিযুত place) · C5 four-operation on large numbers + decimals to thousandths (সহস্রাংশ). A lesson uses numbers **within its class's ceiling** (rule M-1 deeper-not-faster).
- **Operation symbols:** `+ − × ÷ =` introduced with their concept (`+`/`=` at C1, `−` at C1, `×` at C2, `÷` at C3); plus `<` `>` for comparison, brackets at C4 (গাণিতিক বাক্য ৫.১).
- **Math terms (the controlled term list per chapter):** যোগফল, বিয়োগফল, গুণফল, ভাগফল, ভাজ্য/ভাজক/ভাগফল/ভাগশেষ (C4 division), স্থানীয় মান (একক·দশক·শতক·সহস্র…), গুণিতক ও গুণনীয়ক, মৌলিক/যৌগিক সংখ্যা, ল.সা.গু / গ.সা.গু (C5), ভগ্নাংশ (লব/হর, সমতুল), দশমিক ভগ্নাংশ, শতকরা (লাভ-ক্ষতি), পরিমাপ (দৈর্ঘ্য/ওজন/আয়তন), পরিসীমা ও ক্ষেত্রফল, ঘনবস্তু, কোণ/ত্রিভুজ/চতুর্ভুজ/বৃত্ত, সারণি ও চিত্রলেখ/স্তম্ভলেখ (data). The required-term list for any lesson is its TG chapter terms **plus** the symbols **plus** any earlier-tier term it reuses.

**Rules (Math-specific, in addition to REF-02 §2.2 rules):**
1. **Rule M-1** (dependency-ladder order) is non-negotiable; a lesson may not assume a higher rung than its class has taught, and stays within the year's number ceiling.
2. **Rule M-2** (strand ≠ chapter): the যোগ্যতা code comes from the chapter header, not the chapter number.
3. **Rule M-3**: respect the NCF resequencing (division/fractions from C3; LCM/GCD at C5) — bridge with Flex, never Spine deletion.
4. **CPA**: introduce concrete → pictorial → abstract; do not open on the bare algorithm. **The CPA stage is labelled in the lesson plan** — each teaching point and Example-Bank entry tagged (C) / (P) / (A), and the Lesson Flow names its stage — so the teacher understands and applies the sequence, not only the author.
5. **F-01** holds on every word-problem context (no Western names/foods/clothing — names from REF-20).

### 2.3 Bloom's-Level Distribution — Math per class band

- **Class 1–2 band** (from REF-02 §2.3 / REF-06 §3.6): Remember 35–45%, Understand 30–40%, Apply 15–25%, Analyze 0–10%, Evaluate 0–5%, Create 0–5%. **Number-fact and procedural-fluency lessons** (number recognition, the 2–10 tables, the carry/borrow algorithm) may be **Remember/Apply-dominant with justification** — fluency is a legitimate Math outcome. But the **pattern and reasoning lessons reach into Analyze/Create even at C1–C2** and are explicitly permitted there (they are the PROTECTED strand — §3.4).
- **Class 3–5 bands:** use **REF-06 §3.6** for the exact indicative shares (the recall/procedure share falls and Analyze/Evaluate/Create rise as the class rises). Not re-printed here to avoid drift; read them from REF-06 when C3–5 production begins.

> **Math Bloom rule — the procedure-vs-reasoning split.** A Math chapter is rarely one Bloom emphasis. Set the §2.3 mix from the **lesson type**, not the chapter title:
> - **Computation / algorithm lessons** (carry/borrow, long multiplication, long division, decimal operations) → Remember + Apply, with one Understand check ("why does carrying work?").
> - **Word-problem / four-operation lessons** (`MATH-FOUROP`) → Understand + **Apply + Analyze** (choose the operation, justify the steps).
> - **Reasoning & pattern lessons** (`MATH-SENTENCE`, `MATH-PATTERN`) → **Analyze + Create** (investigate, build, justify) — protect this reach even when the topic's structural load is Low.

### 2.4 Exit-Check — Math forms

## §3 — The Math Flex Zone & pedagogical posture (specializing REF-02 §4)

### 3.1 What a Math teacher MAY / MAY NOT decide
Inherits REF-02 §4.1/§4.2 unchanged. Math-specific points:
- **MAY:** choose which §3.2 Example-Bank instance illustrates a concept; choose a §3.3 discovery pattern; pick the concrete manipulative; reorder Guided/Independent practice; add an extra estimation or "find-the-mistake" warm-up; add a Flex-Zone LCM/GCD bridge at C4 if the school values it (rule M-3).
- **MAY NOT:** skip a dependency-ladder rung or pre-teach above the class's number ceiling (**rule M-1**); read a strand off the chapter number (**rule M-2**); open a new concept on the bare algorithm (skip the CPA concrete rung); replace a reasoning/pattern lesson's open task with closed drill; teach a NEEDS-REPLACEMENT illustration as KEEP-AS-IS; (new/probationary teachers) reverse Hook ↔ Direct-Instruction.

### 3.2 Math Example Bank conventions (REF-02 §4.4)
**Form for Math = worked examples / problem instances / manipulative set-ups**, minimum 3 per teaching point.
- Examples are concrete and age-banded, drawn from the §2.2(B) number ceiling and term list where possible (so examples double as required-fact practice), and follow the CPA order (at least one concrete, one pictorial, one abstract per High-load teaching point). **Each example is tagged (C) / (P) / (A) in the lesson plan** so the teacher delivers them in sequence and learns the CPA habit (§2.2).
- All example **contexts** are F-01-clean and curation-clean; Islamic-resonant contexts are welcome `[Islamic-add]` — e.g. counting **dates / rak'ahs of salah / pages of the Qur'an**; sharing sweets **among siblings** or **sadaqah** among the needy (division); **honest trade & profit** for money/percentage (no interest); **prayer times / Ramadan days** for time; data from **the class, the garden, the masjid**.
- For any living-being counting example, use the **object or the word only**, not a picture (C-05); count fruit, books, shapes, stars rather than animals or people where possible.
- The author lists the bank per teaching point in the lesson plan; the teacher picks or substitutes (substitution logged for the Subject Lead).

### 3.3 Math Discovery-Pattern library (REF-02 §4.5)
Minimum 3 per lesson; concrete enough for a new teacher to run unaided. The canonical Math patterns:

1. **Manipulative-led** — hand out counters / place-value blocks / fraction bars; students model the concept before the symbol (Hook; CPA concrete rung).
2. **Number-line / visual-model-led** — students place or move on a number line, draw the bar/array model (CPA pictorial rung).
3. **Pattern-investigation (প্যাটার্ন খুঁজি)** — show a number or shape sequence; "what comes next, **and why**?" (the free-thinking pattern; `MATH-PATTERN`, PROTECTED).
4. **Estimation-first (আগে আন্দাজ করি)** — students estimate before computing, then check — builds number sense and self-checking.
5. **Story-problem-led (গল্পের অঙ্ক)** — pose a real **halal** context (honest trade, sharing, measuring for salah); students identify the operation (`MATH-FOUROP`).
6. **Error-analysis (ভুল খুঁজি)** — show a worked sum with a planted mistake; students find and explain it (Analyze; checking-by-inverse).
7. **Build-your-own (নিজে বানাই)** — students create their own sum / pattern / data question for a partner (the self-discovery pattern; `MATH-SENTENCE` reasoning facet).
8. **Paired-exploration (জোড়ায় কাজ)** — students work a problem in pairs before whole-class share (the NCF collaborative manner; same-gender pairs per C-02).
9. **Sort-and-classify (সাজাই / শ্রেণিবিন্যাস)** — sort number/shape cards (odd/even, prime/composite, shape families) — for `MATH-ODDEVEN`, `MATH-FACTORS`, `MATH-GEOMETRY`.
10. **Measure-the-real-thing (নিজে মাপি)** — students measure real objects/lengths/containers, collect and chart the data (`MATH-MEASURE`, `MATH-DATA`; the inquiry/group framing NCF emphasises).

### 3.4 Free thinking within an Islamic framing — the PROTECTED competencies

*(This section operationalizes Instructions §6 for Math and folds in the free-thinking findings from REF-19's Math fill — third-fill calibration item 2.)*

REF-19's load-bearing metric scores these two topics **Low on dependency**, which would wrongly read as "safe to compress." REF-19 §4A tags them **🟦 PROTECTED**; this playbook treats them as **never the first to cut when time is short**:

| PROTECTED competency | Why protected | Where it lives across the classes |
|---|---|---|
| **Number & shape patterns (`MATH-PATTERN`, প্যাটার্ন)** | the clearest free-thinking vehicle in Math — "investigate, create and **justify** patterns" (যৌক্তিক চিন্তা / যুক্তিবাদী ও সৃজনশীল) | **threaded across all five classes** — C1 U13 (১.৬+৬.৬, the only two-competency C1 chapter, NCF-new), C2 (num U01 + shape U04), C3 U01, C4 (num U01 + geom U10), C5 (geom U09). Never the first to compress; use open "what comes next, and why?" tasks. |
| **Mathematical sentences & reasoning (`MATH-SENTENCE`, গাণিতিক বাক্য)** | the pre-algebra reasoning seed — build/interpret math sentences (brackets), **analyse and justify relationships among quantities** | NCF introduces it at **C4 (U05, ৫.১)** and deepens it at **C5 (U02, ৫.১)**. **Deeper-not-faster for the reasoning/pattern strand:** when a class is ahead, enrich with **richer patterns, multi-step reasoning, and harder real contexts within the year's number ceiling** — do **not** formalise pattern/reasoning into early algebra notation, and do **not** accelerate into the next class's Spine (REF-19 §1.4). The two C5 **review rungs** (`MATH-FRACTION` ৩.৩, `MATH-MEASURE` ৭.১) are the model: deepen and consolidate, don't race ahead.

**Origin note (TG-confirmed):** the reasoning/pattern strand is **native to NCF** at every class (প্যাটার্ন is NCF-introduced; গাণিতিক বাক্য is the NCF reframing of the old "গাণিতিক প্রতীক"). Emphasising free-thinking aligns the school's mission *with* NCF intent, not against it.

### 3.5 Redirection patterns (the discovery-stays-Islamic move)
REF-02 §4.3 defers redirection patterns to REF-03. In Math the redirect is almost always a **context swap**, not a content correction — preserve the child's numeracy and fix only the framing, **without shaming**:
- *A student's own word problem uses a lottery / prize-bond / "win money by chance" setting* → "সুন্দর অঙ্ক! এবার টাকাটা সৎ ব্যবসায় লাভ হিসেবে ধরি" — keep the numbers, move to honest trade/sharing (C-06 / C-08).
- *A student frames a money problem around earning interest (সুদ)* → keep the saving/amount, replace the "extra for lending" with honest profit or a gift (C-08).
- *A student counts hero portraits / national symbols / flowers-at-the-monument* → "চমৎকার গণনা! এবার আমরা বই / ফল / তারা গুনি তো" — swap to neutral counting objects (E-18c).
- *A student's context uses a Western name / food* → redirect to a REF-20 name and a local halal food (C-10 / F-01).
- *A student personifies a number/tool as deciding ("ক্যালকুলেটর ঠিক করল…")* → re-anchor that **we** reason and Allah gave us the ability (rare in Math; C-09).
The redirection preserves the child's initiative (the free-thinking value) and corrects only the framing. These patterns also seed Project 07 teacher training.

## §5 — Per-class emphasis digest

This is a **digest**, not a re-print — REF-19 v1.9 (Math section) remains the source of truth for the 24-topic map, the load-bearing tiers, and the §4A PROTECTED tags. For each class it answers the lesson-author's two questions: *what must be mastered this year (load-bearing)?* and *what must be protected this year regardless of structural weight (PROTECTED)?* — under the "deeper-not-faster" guardrail (REF-19 §1.4: ahead-of-pace classes go deeper in the same topic within the year's number ceiling; never accelerate into the next class's Spine).

**Cross-class vertical anchors (emphasise the through-line, not just the year):**
- **The operations ladder** (count → place value → add/sub → multiply → divide) is the High-load backbone; its early rungs — **C1 place value (NCF-new U10)**, **C2 multiplication tables**, **C3 division** — are the highest-value mastery checkpoints in the whole subject (rule M-1).
- **The rational-number chain** (fractions C3 → decimals C4 → percentage C5) runs upward; the **C5 review rungs** (fractions ৩.৩, measurement ৭.১) are **built-in C4↔C5 vertical anchors** — use them to diagnose C4 gaps before building C5 content.
- **Pattern & reasoning (`MATH-PATTERN` C1→C5; `MATH-SENTENCE` C4→C5)** is the school's free-thinking competency in Math and is **🟦 PROTECTED at every class it appears.**
- **Money** is a **standalone chapter C1–C3, then applied C4–C5** (COVERAGE-thin) — keep money fluency alive through applied tasks in the upper years; never a Spine deletion.

### 5.1 — Class 1 emphasis
**Master (🔴 High):** counting & number sense (to 100, incl. zero & order), **place value (U10 — NCF-new, the closed PREREQ-GAP)**, addition, subtraction, geometry (plane shapes), with compare (🟠) the conceptual gateway and money (🟠) introduced. These are the foundation the entire stack rests on. **Protect (🟦):** **pattern (U13)** — the only two-competency C1 chapter (number ১.৬ + shape ৬.৬), NCF-introduced; run open "what comes next, and why?" tasks. **Deeper-not-faster:** an advanced C1 class deepens *within* 100 — number-line work, real-object story sums, richer patterns — and **must not pre-teach two-digit addition** (that is C2 Spine, rule M-1). **Handoff:** confirm place-value mastery before the C2 carrying step.

### 5.2 — Class 2 emphasis
**Master (🔴 High):** **multiplication STARTS here** (tables 2–10, ×0/×1 🆕, commutativity) — the C2/C3 investment that pays across the upper years; addition (carry) and subtraction (borrow) with the add↔sub algorithm; place value to শতক; measurement (length/weight/volume); geometry. **Rule M-3 watch:** **division and fractions are NOT taught at C2** — they begin at C3; brief teachers so no one assumes the Era-2 placement. **New:** data handling (U07, ৮.১ — wholly NCF-new). **Protect (🟦):** **pattern** (number U01 + shape U04 🆕). **Handoff:** confirm C1 place-value mastery before carrying; confirm tables fluency before C3 division.

### 5.3 — Class 3 emphasis
**Master (🔴 High):** **division BEGINS** (U06, ২.৫ — concept via sharing, remainder); **fractions BEGIN** (U09, ৩.১); multiplication deepens (tables to 15); the explicit **add↔sub (U04)** and **mul↔div (U07)** relationship chapters (NCF-new — teach checking-by-inverse); four-operation word problems (U08); measurement; geometry (lines/angles, protractor); money's **last standalone chapter** (U10). **Protect (🟦):** **pattern** (U01). **Curation watch:** the data chapter (U13) carries a **cockfighting / fancy-dress pictograph context** (→ C-06 / C-19) and pictograph living-being icons (→ C-05) — see §2.5; the maths is clean, the context/illustration is the replacement work.

### 5.4 — Class 4 emphasis
**Master (🔴 High):** multi-digit multiplication (×99/×1000 short methods) and **long division** (ভাজ্য/ভাজক/ভাগফল/ভাগশেষ); number theory (U06 — primes/composites/divisibility); fractions (proper/improper/mixed); **decimals begin** (U08); **measurement is the largest chapter** (U09 — ৭.৩ perimeter & area; time stays inside পরিমাপ ৭.৪, *not* dropped — the corrected reading); geometry (U10). **New & PROTECTED (🟦):** **mathematical sentences** (U05, ৫.১) — the NCF reframing of "গাণিতিক প্রতীক," the pre-algebra reasoning seed; protect it. **Rule M-2 watch:** Ch6 গুণিতক ও গুণনীয়ক is strand **২.৮**, not "৬.x." **Rule M-3:** LCM/GCD is **deferred to C5** — add a C4 Flex-Zone bridge only if the school values early LCM/GCD. **Money** goes **applied** (no standalone chapter). **Protect (🟦):** math sentences (NEW) + pattern.

### 5.5 — Class 5 emphasis
**Master (🔴 High):** four operations on large numbers (U01); **LCM/GCD lands** (U03, ২.৮); fractions (U04 — **opens with 2 review পাঠ ৩.৩, the C4 anchor** — use it to diagnose C4 gaps); **decimals to thousandths** (U05 — the largest C5 chapter, all four operations); **measurement** (U08 — area via formula; **reviews C4 ৭.১**; the largest chapter by পাঠ); geometry (U09 — circle & parts, **3-D solids ৬.৭ NEW**). **New strands to track to C6:** **average** (U07, ২.৯), **percentage** (U06, ৩.৬). **Alignment asset:** the percentage chapter teaches profit/loss **with no interest / সুদ** — Islamic-favourable (§2.5, Project 02 positive register). **Protect (🟦):** **mathematical sentences & reasoning** (U02, ৫.১ — analyse and justify relationships among quantities) and **pattern** (U09, geometric). **Forward:** average, percentage, 3-D solids, and graphs → track downward as Class 6 arrives (append-only, REF-19 §3.2).

## §6 — Author workflow, before-publishing checklist & content-writer guidance

### 6.1 The Math lesson-plan author workflow (stepwise)
1. **Locate** the chapter (অধ্যায়) in the class Math skeleton v2 (REF-05); pull its TG-validated যোগ্যতা/শিখনফল + period count from the matching `completed_C{n}_MATH_TGReconciliation_v1`. **Read the strand from the chapter header (rule M-2).**
2. **Decide the production stage** (§0 step 0 / D-050): single-period Session Plan, or Chapter Plan + Session Plans (session count from the TG period count, within the ±40% / ±1 band).
3. **Classify** the chapter's dependency-ladder rung (§2.2A) and REF-19 topic + tier.
4. **Write the Spine** field-by-field using §2 (objective from যোগ্যতা; must-cover from the rung + number ceiling + term list, in CPA order; Bloom mix from §2.3 incl. the procedure-vs-reasoning split; exit-check from §2.4/§3.6).
5. **Curate** with §2.5: scan the high-incidence categories first (it is a **picture-and-context scan** in Math); tag KEEP/REPLACE/FLEXIBLE; apply F-01/F-03/F-05; for NEEDS-REPLACEMENT, hand the preserved-items note to Project 05 (`RC-MATH-C{n}-U{nn}`).
6. **Revision** — copy the topic's row off the §4 chart into §2.6.c (do not invent).
7. **Flex Zone** — provide a §4.4 Example Bank (≥3, CPA-ordered) from §3.2 and §4.5 Discovery Patterns (≥3) from §3.3.
8. **Homework** — §2.7/§2.8 within REF-08 caps; spaced mixed practice for fluency; Pool `QP-MATH-C{n}-U{nn}`; topic tag `TOP-MATH-C{n}-{nn}`.
9. **Emphasis** — check §5: load-bearing → emphasise mastery; 🟦 PROTECTED → never the first to cut; respect deeper-not-faster.
10. **Run the §6.2 checklist** before submitting for review.

### 6.2 Before-publishing checklist (Math — extends REF-02 §7)
- [ ] §2.1 objective uses an observable Math verb and cites the chapter's NCF যোগ্যতা code (read from the **header**, not the chapter number — rule M-2).

- [ ] PROTECTED competency present in this lesson (pattern / math-sentence)? If so, it is emphasised, not compressed, and not formalised beyond the class's level.

### 6.3 Content-writer adaptation guidance (for Project 05 replacement content)
*(Math is values-neutral — the replacement work is almost entirely **context and illustration**, not the mathematics. Keep the numeracy outcome, required numbers, symbols, and terms; change only the flesh.)*
- **What to keep, what to change.** Keep the chapter's যোগ্যতা/outcome, the numbers and operations, the difficulty, and the required terms. Change only the **word-problem context** and the **illustration**.
- **Preferred halal contexts (replace neutral or problematic ones).** Counting **dates / rak'ahs of salah / pages of the Qur'an**; sharing sweets or sadaqah **among siblings or the needy** (division); **honest trade and profit** — buying and selling, no interest (money, percentage, four-op); **prayer times, Ramadan days, the Hijri calendar** (time); measuring **for the masjid, the garden, the home** (measurement); data **from the class, the masjid, the vegetable garden** (data handling).
- **Money & interest (C-08).** Neutral money, saving, and honest buying/selling are **acceptable as-is**. Replace **only** an interest *return* ("repaid with extra for lending") with honest profit, a gift, or qard hasan (interest-free loan repaid in the same amount). The C5 percentage chapter is already riba-free — keep it that way.
- **Illustrations (C-05 / C-02 / C-18).** Count and chart **objects** (fruit, books, shapes, stars), not living beings; if a figure is unavoidable, render it **faceless**; depict only same-gender or mahram (family) groupings; **swap hero-portrait / national-symbol counting items for neutral objects** (E-18c) and remove monument-floral/veneration framing.
- **Contexts to avoid.** Gambling / lottery / prize-bond / dice-staking (C-06); interest returns (C-08); Western names/foods/clothing (C-10 / F-01 — names from **REF-20**, matching class pool); music/dance counting items (C-03/C-16 — swap for a clean counting noun, F-05); "act as the shopkeeper" role-play (C-19 — use genuine same-gender paired practice instead).
