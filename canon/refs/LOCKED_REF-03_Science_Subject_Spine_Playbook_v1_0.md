## §0 — Read-First Summary & Checklist (staff-facing)

**What this playbook is.** REF-02 says *what every lesson plan must contain* (the 8-field Spine, the Lesson Flow, the Flex Zone). REF-03 says *what those fields mean for Science specifically* — which content is mandatory, which Bloom mix fits each class, which examples and discovery patterns are pre-approved, when each topic is revised, where each year's emphasis goes, and how Science's curation watch-items are handled.

**What it is NOT.** It is not a lesson plan, not a curriculum, and not a re-print of REF-19. It is the subject rulebook a Science lesson-plan author reads *before* writing a single chapter.

**The one Science principle.** *Science is a branching web of inquiry, not a ladder of recall; protect the four clean ladders, run everything else by observation, and keep tawhid the silent frame.* Four strands carry the structural load (the **Force ladder**, the **Astro ladder**, and the **Matter** and **Plant** substrates — 🔴 High); the other strands are shallow 1–2-rung branches. Across all of them, Science's **native pedagogy is already inquiry-led** (Predict–Observe–Explain, একাকী চিন্তা, যুক্তি-পাল্টাযুক্তি) — the school's free-thinking priority is built into the subject, not grafted on.

**The author's 8-step checklist (run before writing any Science chapter — at the Chapter-Plan stage per REF-02 v1.3):**

### 2.1 Learning Objective — Science verbs + the NCF 7-strand যোগ্যতা framework

REF-02 §2.1 requires one observable verb matching the §2.3 Bloom level. For Science:

- **Observable Science verb modes** (pick the mode the objective is checkable in): observe (লক্ষ্য করবে, পর্যবেক্ষণ করবে), classify (শ্রেণিবদ্ধ করবে, আলাদা করবে), investigate (পরীক্ষা করবে, যাচাই করবে, পরিমাপ করবে), explain/reason (ব্যাখ্যা করবে, কারণ বলবে, যুক্তি দেখাবে), predict/infer (অনুমান করবে, পূর্বানুমান করবে — the POE verb), apply/make (তৈরি করবে, সমাধান করবে, ব্যবহার করবে).
- **The NCF অর্জন-উপযোগী যোগ্যতা framework is the objective vocabulary.** Science carries **seven NCF যোগ্যতা strands** (one per REF-19 header). Every Science chapter carries one or more strand codes + 3-part শিখনফল codes; the author **takes the objective from the chapter's TG-validated codes** rather than inventing one:

### 2.2 Must-Cover Content — the topic rung for *this* class

This is the Science form of REF-02 §2.2 (D-021). Unlike Bangla's single decoding ladder, Science Must-Cover Content is **per-topic and per-class**: the topic's REF-19 rung gives the exact depth this class must cover, and the four 🔴 High-load topics are the non-negotiable spine.

**(A) The four load-bearing spines (🔴 High — get these solid; each higher rung strictly presupposes the one below):**

| Spine | Rung sequence (C3 → C4 → C5) | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Force ladder** (`SCI-FORCE`, F) | force changes **shape & volume** (C3 ৪.১) → force changes **motion** (C4 ৪.১) → **friction / gravity / simple machines** (C5 ৪.১/৪.২) | the map's cleanest 3-rung ladder; never pre-teach a rung |
| **Astro ladder** (`SCI-ASTRO`, S) | **the Sun** as light/heat/energy (C3 ৬.১) → **solar system** (C4 ৬.১) → **Earth's motion** (C5 ৬.১) | tawhid-safe framing is mandatory at every rung (§6) |
| **Matter substrate** (`SCI-MATTER`, P) | macroscopic states incl. air-as-matter + magnetism (C3 ৩.১/৩.২) → properties / chemical change / states (C4 ৩.১–৩.৩) → **particle level** atoms/molecules/mixtures (C5 ৩.১/৩.২) | confirm the macroscopic picture before the C5 particle step |
| **Plant substrate** (`SCI-PLANT`, L) | external structure & organ function (C3 ১.১) → germination & growth (C4 ১.১) → (within C5 ecology) | C4 growth is new ground, not a re-run of C3 structure |

**(B) The required content** for any chapter = the topic's REF-19 depth tag for this class (§4 / §5) + the chapter's core terms + any prerequisite rung the chapter genuinely reuses (REF-19 prerequisites column). The author lists the chapter's core terms in the Chapter Plan; replacement examples must reuse them (F-01-clean — §2.5).

**Rules (Science-specific, in addition to REF-02 §2.2 rules):**
1. **Rule S-2 — deepen, don't restart.** A spiral topic's higher rung is genuine new ground (matter: macroscopic → particle; force: deformation → motion → friction). Pitch the chapter at *this* class's rung; do not re-teach the lower rung as if new, and do not borrow the higher rung.
2. **Rule S-3 — observe first.** Wherever the topic permits, the Must-Cover content is anchored in a **real observation or hands-on demo** (a real plant, a magnet, a push/pull, a soil sample) before the term is named. Science's native POE pedagogy makes this the default, not an enrichment.
3. **Rule S-4 — F-01 holds** on every example, name, and context (no Western names/foods/clothing; local/Muslim contexts).
4. **Rule S-5 — materials (D-049).** Teacher writes on **হোয়াইটবোর্ড + মার্কার**; students use **খাতা / পেনসিল / কলম**; **no স্লেট, no চক** anywhere. Hands-on Science উপকরণ (magnets, seeds, etc.) are listed separately in §2.6 materials-to-bag; the **C4 ICT/Scratch chapter raises a device-needs flag** (§5.2).

## §3 — How a Science teacher works (specializing REF-02 §4)

### 3.1 What a Science teacher MAY / MAY NOT decide
Inherits REF-02 §4.1/§4.2. Science-specific:
- **MAY:** choose which §3.2 Example-Bank instance illustrates a concept; choose a §3.3 discovery pattern; reorder Guided/Independent practice; substitute an equivalent local, halal, F-01-clean observation; choose the hands-on উপকরণ.
- **MAY NOT:** pre-teach a higher rung of a 🔴 ladder than the class has reached (rule S-2); replace a hands-on observation with a verbal description where the objective is observe/investigate; teach a NEEDS-REPLACEMENT image as KEEP-AS-IS; reframe the Sun / Earth's-motion away from the tawhid-safe science-only framing; (new/probationary teachers) reverse Hook ↔ Direct-Instruction.

### 3.2 Science Example Bank conventions (REF-02 §4.4)
**Form for Science = concrete instances / observations / worked phenomena**, minimum 3 per teaching point.

### 3.3 Science Discovery-Pattern library (REF-02 §4.5)
Minimum 3 per chapter; concrete enough for a new teacher to run unaided. The canonical Science patterns (Science's native inquiry pedagogy is the gift here):

1. **Predict–Observe–Explain (POE) — অনুমান → পর্যবেক্ষণ → ব্যাখ্যা.** Ask students to predict, then do/show the phenomenon, then explain the gap. The Science flagship pattern (Hook + investigation).
2. **Real-object observation (বাস্তব বস্তু পর্যবেক্ষণ).** Bring the real thing (plant, magnet, soil, water) — observe before naming (rule S-3).
3. **Sort-and-justify (শ্রেণিবদ্ধ করো ও কারণ বলো).** Students sort instances (living/non-living, float/sink) and **justify the rule** — a strong inquiry vehicle for `SCI-CLASSIFY`.
4. **Demonstration-led (পরীক্ষণ/ডেমো).** Teacher runs a safe demo; students record what changed (force, magnetism, states of matter).
5. **Question-led (কেন / কী হলে কী হতো).** Open question before content; students hypothesise (the একাকী চিন্তা moment).
6. **Argue–counter-argue (যুক্তি-পাল্টাযুক্তি).** Paired/small-group: one offers a claim, another a counter — NCF-native reasoning manner; oracy + analysis.
7. **Investigate-and-measure (পরিমাপ করে দেখি).** Simple measurement/comparison (which dissolves faster, which is heavier) — links to Math measurement (cross-curricular).
8. **Design-your-own-solution (নিজের সমাধান বানাই).** Open computational/engineering task — the `SCI-ICT` self-try flagship (sequential instructions → block-code → problem-solving). **PROTECTED — keep open-ended.**
9. **Nature-walk / collection (প্রকৃতি থেকে সংগ্রহ).** Students collect/observe outdoors and bring back data (living things, leaves, weather notes) — homework-friendly.
10. **Systems-trace (নির্ভরশীলতার জাল আঁকি).** Students trace a web of dependence / energy flow — the `SCI-ECOLOGY` C5 capstone. **PROTECTED — keep the reasoning task.**

### 3.5 Redirection patterns (the discovery-stays-Islamic move)
When a student's hypothesis, example, or own-way answer diverges from Islamic values, the teacher redirects **without shaming**:
- *Student personifies the sun/rain/nature as the giver of life* → "কে দিয়েছেন সূর্যের আলো ও তাপ?" — re-anchor causation in Allah while keeping the scientific observation (C-09; the tawhid frame).
- *Student offers a song/tune as a memory aid for a process* → keep the rhythm/sequence, drop the melody (C-03).
- *Student names a Western figure/brand as a technology example* → redirect to a familiar local/halal example (C-10 / F-01).
- *Student's own design/solution drifts into a haram context (gambling app, music device)* → acknowledge the effort, redirect the application to a permissible one (a prayer-time reminder, a watering scheduler) while keeping the child's computational idea.
These preserve the child's initiative (the free-thinking value) and correct only the framing. They also seed Project 07 teacher training.
