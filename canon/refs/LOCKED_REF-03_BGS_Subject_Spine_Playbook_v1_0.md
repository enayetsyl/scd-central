## §0 — Read-First Summary & Checklist (staff-facing)

**What this playbook is.** REF-02 says *what every lesson plan must contain* (the 8-field Spine, the Lesson Flow, the Flex Zone, now split into a Chapter Plan + Session Plans). REF-03-BGS says *what those fields mean for BGS specifically* — which content is mandatory, which place-names and civic terms must appear, which Bloom mix fits each class, which curation categories bite hardest, when each topic is revised, and where each year's emphasis goes.

**What it is NOT.** It is not a lesson plan, not a curriculum, and not a re-print of REF-19. It is the subject rulebook a BGS lesson-plan author reads *before* writing a single lesson.

**The one BGS principle.** *BGS is structurally light-load but mission-heavy — and the heaviest curation work in the school lives here.* Unlike Bangla (a decoding ladder) or Math (a number ladder), BGS has **no single High-load spine** — geography does not depend on history, ethics does not depend on professions. Only `BGS-COUNTRY` carries real substrate load (it feeds five "of-Bangladesh" topics). What the structural metric *cannot* see is where the school's mission actually sits: in **akhlaq formation** (`BGS-ETHICS`, the one PROTECTED topic) and in the **curation of the highest-density subject in the whole curriculum**. So the BGS author spends less time sequencing prerequisites and more time on two things: (1) emphasising the akhlaq anchor and the school's free-thinking inquiry posture, and (2) curating — music/dance/festivals, other-faith content, the restored leaders chapter, ethnic-cultural traits, gender framing, and pervasive mixed-gender illustrations. Plan more Project-02 / Project-05 throughput for BGS than for any other subject.

**The author's 9-step checklist (run before writing any BGS lesson plan — *or, under REF-02 v1.3, a Chapter Plan + its Session Plans*):**

Session count = the chapter's TG-Reconciliation period count, within REF-02 §2A.3's ±40% / ±1 band; set **once**, at chapter-planning. ## §1 — Purpose, scope, and how REF-03-BGS fits

### 1.1 Purpose
REF-03-BGS turns the universal lesson-plan template (REF-02) into something a BGS author can execute without guessing. It answers, for BGS and for each of Classes 3–5: what must be covered, in what cognitive mix, with what civic vocabulary and place-names, under what Islamic-curation rules (the heaviest in the school), on what revision schedule, with what examples and discovery patterns, and with what year-on-year emphasis.

### 1.2 Scope
- **In scope:** BGS, Classes 3–5 — the full BGS vertical stack as the subject currently exists in the school.
- **Ragged start:** there are **no Class 1–2 rungs** — BGS begins at Class 3 (REF-19 §6). The playbook is therefore three-class, not five-class.
- **Out of scope:** other subjects (each has its own REF-03+ playbook); Class 6+ (absorbed later — REF-19 is append-only and this playbook will follow when the C6 BGS analysis lands). Expect C6 to introduce formal civics/government and possibly the first systematic constitutional-rights treatment (NCTB Playbook §15 prediction).

### 1.3 The framework facts that anchor the whole playbook (TG-corrected)
**All three BGS classes' 2026 target editions are NCF 2021 — TG-confirmed per class.** A pupil's C3 → C4 → C5 path is one continuous NCF path. Three operational facts the author must hold:

1. **The NCF boundary *year* differs by class** (C3 broke at 2024; C4 and C5 broke at 2025→2026), but the **2026 target is uniformly NCF** for all three. **No framework zigzag for BGS.** Always confirm the boundary per class; never assume the C3 timing applies upward.
2. **Chapter order ≠ strand order** throughout BGS. A chapter's যোগ্যতা strand is read from the chapter's own header, not from its position. Examples: C5 Ch9 carries strand 5 (rights); C5 Ch15 carries strand 9 (banking); C4 Ch10 carries dual code ৭.১ + ৭.২; C5 Ch2 carries dual code ২.১ + ২.২. **Take the objective from the header strand, not the chapter number** (§2.1, checklist step 2).
3. **The C3 TG-vs-2026 offset = −1.** The C3 BGS Teacher Guide was finalised against the 13-chapter **2025** textbook; the September 2025 reprint dropped TG-Ch3 (আমাদের চার নেতা) entirely, renumbering Ch4–13 to Ch3–12 in the 12-chapter 2026 book. **For C3, shift Ch4 onward by −1 when reading the TG against the 2026 textbook.** A print-and-keep offset table sits in the C3 BGS Skeleton v2 (Project 01). **C4 and C5 have no offset** (their TGs are 1:1 with their 2026 textbooks).

## §2 — The BGS Spine (specializing REF-02 §2)

REF-02's Spine has eight numbered fields. This section gives the BGS form of each. Fields REF-02 already fully specifies (§2.4 mechanics, §2.7/§2.8 homework structure) carry only BGS notes; the fields REF-02 defers to the Subject Spine Playbook (§2.2, §2.3) carry the substantive specialization.

### 2.1 Learning Objective — BGS verbs + the NCF 10-strand যোগ্যতা framework

REF-02 §2.1 requires one observable verb matching the §2.3 Bloom level. For BGS:

- **Observable BGS verb modes** (pick the mode the objective is checkable in): oral/explain (বলবে, ব্যাখ্যা করবে, বর্ণনা করবে), identify/locate (চিহ্নিত করবে, মানচিত্রে দেখাবে, খুঁজে বের করবে), classify/compare (শ্রেণিবদ্ধ করবে, তুলনা করবে), apply/practise (অনুশীলন করবে, পালন করবে — for rights/safety/ethics behaviours), and the free-thinking modes (নিজের মতামত দেবে, যুক্তি দেখাবে, বিচার করবে — opinion / reasoning / judgement).
- **The NCF 10-strand অর্জন-উপযোগী যোগ্যতা framework is the objective vocabulary.** Every NCF BGS chapter carries one (sometimes two) strand codes and 3-part শিখনফল codes; the author **takes the objective from the chapter's TG-validated codes** — read from the header, since chapter ≠ strand (§1.3). ### 2.2 Must-Cover Content — the strand content + the ladders + required terms

This is the BGS form of REF-02 §2.2 (D-021). BGS Must-Cover has three parts: **(A)** the chapter's NCF strand content, **(B)** the **load-bearing position** of the topic (which ladder it sits on, if any), and **(C)** the required **place-names / civic terms / dates**.

**(A) Strand content.** Taken from the chapter header strand + its 3-part শিখনফল (read at authoring time from the TGR).

**(B) Load-bearing position — the BGS ladders.** BGS is structurally **flat** (only one High topic), so most lessons are *terminal* (no downstream dependent — teach for mastery in-year, not as a prerequisite). The exceptions are the four through-lines the author must honour with "deeper-not-faster":

| Ladder / spine | Rungs (C3 → C4 → C5) | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **`BGS-COUNTRY` substrate 🔴 High** | location & 64 districts (C3) → landforms & rivers (C4) → communication maps (C5) | The one High-load topic; **feeds PRODUCTS, FOREST, RESOURCES, POPULATION, INTERORG (5 dependents).** Confirm C3 district mastery before the C4 river system, and C4 mastery before the C5 maps step. |
| **Rights ladder (R) 🟠** | family role (C3) → child rights & road safety (C3) → citizen rights & duties (C4) → state/society + leadership (C5) | Rights vocabulary spirals outward family → society → state. **The C5 rung packs four sub-strands (৫.১–৫.৪) into one chapter — the most strand-dense chapter in BGS; deeper-not-faster is critical, do not compress.** |
| **Money ladder (F) 🟢→🟢** | money use & need-vs-want (C3) → economical use & savings habit (C4) → personal budget & banking (C5) | Wholly NCF-new strand (no Era-A money chapter). A clean 3-rung spiral: use → conserve → budget. The C5 banking rung integrates a **halal-finance hook** (no riba in the NCF baseline — see §2.5). |
| **Depth-spirals (same code, deepening framing)** | ENV: environment → natural & social → **climate change** (C5) · ETHICS: interpersonal → daily-practice → **tolerance** · COEXIST: general → religious → people-and-diversity · GEOGRAPHY: world → continent → region (a "narrowing zoom") | These are *depth-shifts at the same যোগ্যতা code*, not new topics. Confirm the prior framing is solid before the deeper one. |

> **Rule BGS-1 — the strand-presence facts.** Two BGS-unique structural facts the author must not paper over:
> - **`DROP-CROSS-CLASS` — Professions (strand 8) is present at C3 + C4 and ABSENT at C5.** Do not invent a C5 professions lesson; the strand genuinely drops. It is a terminal topic (no dependents), so the drop creates no prerequisite gap.
> - **`POLITICAL-VARIABLE` — the leaders chapter is non-monotonic.** Dropped at C3 (2026), absent at C4, **reintroduced as a standalone chapter at C5** (Ch4 আমাদের স্মরণীয় নেতা, যোগ্যতা ৩.১, opening with এ কে ফজলুল হক, শিখনফল ৩.১.৩ "শ্রদ্ধা প্রদর্শন"). The C5 chapter carries the **highest political content of the BGS classes** and is the **highest-priority BGS curation item** (§2.5).

**(C) Required terms (REF-01 §4.2/§4.3 — content-level).** BGS is term-and-place heavy. The required list for any lesson is its TG থিম vocabulary plus the topic's canonical place-names / dates / civic terms — e.g. the 64 districts and neighbouring countries (COUNTRY/GEOGRAPHY); ১৯৪৮ / ১৯৫২ / শহিদ মিনার / ৭ই মার্চ / ২৬শে মার্চ / ১৬ই ডিসেম্বর (LANGMOV/LIBERATION); মহাস্থানগড় / উয়ারী-বটেশ্বর (HISTSITES); SAARC/UN-type organisations (INTERORG). Place-name and date accuracy is non-negotiable.

**Rules (BGS-specific, in addition to REF-02 §2.2 rules):**
1. Take the objective from the **header strand**, never the chapter number (§1.3 fact 2).
2. At **C3**, apply the **−1 TG offset** for Ch4 onward (§1.3 fact 3).
3. Honour the four ladders' order; for terminal topics, teach for in-year mastery, not as a prerequisite.
4. F-01 holds on every example name/food/clothing.

### 2.3 Bloom's-Level Distribution — BGS per class band

- **Class 3–5 bands:** use **REF-06 §3.6** for the exact indicative shares (recall share falls and Analyze/Evaluate/Create rise as the class rises). Not re-printed here to avoid drift; read them from REF-06 when production begins.
- **BGS cognitive character.** BGS leans **Understand + Apply**, but it is the subject most naturally built for an **Analyze / Evaluate reach** — the inquiry pedagogy (observe → individual work → group work → discussion → evaluate) is designed for it. Three topic families should deliberately reach higher Bloom:
  - **Ethics (M):** moral *judgement* (Evaluate) — "is this just? why?" — not recall of a definition.
  - **Rights & citizenship (R):** *application* to the child's own family/school/community context, and at C5 *evaluation* of social problems.
  - **Disaster (D):** *application* — the personal-response role is a do-it skill, not a fact to memorise.

> **BGS Bloom rule — knowledge vs judgement.** Place-names, dates, district lists, and organisation names sit at Remember/Understand. The *ethics, rights, coexistence, and disaster-response* outcomes sit at Apply/Analyze/Evaluate. Set §2.3 from **which kind of outcome the chapter's শিখনফল names**, not from "it's a social-studies chapter."

## §3 — The BGS Flex Zone & pedagogical posture (specializing REF-02 §4)

### 3.1 What a BGS teacher MAY / MAY NOT decide
Inherits REF-02 §4.1/§4.2 unchanged. BGS-specific points:
- **MAY:** choose which §3.2 Example-Bank instance illustrates a strand point; choose a §3.3 discovery pattern; reorder Guided/Independent practice; adapt local examples (the district, the local river, the nearby historic site) to the school's own region (Sylhet) where the strand allows.
- **MAY NOT:** teach a NEEDS-REPLACEMENT element (the leaders-chapter veneration framing, glorified music/dance, devotional other-faith framing) as KEEP-AS-IS; introduce riba into the banking lesson; collapse the inquiry cycle's individual-then-group sequence into teacher-talk; decide a borderline C-18 civic-vs-veneration act alone (route to REF-01 §4.5); (new/probationary teachers) reverse Hook ↔ Direct-Instruction.

### 3.2 BGS Example Bank conventions (REF-02 §4.4)
**Form for BGS = concrete instances** (a district, a product, an occupation, a moral scenario, a place-name, a disaster-response step), minimum 3 per teaching point.
- Examples are concrete, age-banded, drawn from the §2.2(C) required terms where possible (so examples double as required-term practice) and localised to the school's region where the strand allows.
- All examples F-01-clean and curation-clean; Islamic-resonant examples are welcome `[Islamic-add]` (e.g. for ethics: returning a found item / amanah; honouring parents; for professions: the dignity of halal labour; for resources: water as Allah's ni'mah to be conserved).
- For living-being / people examples, prefer the **term or scenario**, not a picture (C-05).
- The author lists the bank per teaching point; the teacher picks or substitutes (substitution logged for the Subject Lead).

### 3.3 BGS Discovery-Pattern library (REF-02 §4.5)
Minimum 3 per lesson; concrete enough for a new teacher to run unaided. The **NCF inquiry cycle is the backbone** and directly serves the school's free-thinking / self-try priority:

1. **Inquiry cycle (the NCF default)** — পর্যবেক্ষণ (observe a picture / map / scenario) → **কাজ-ক (individual work)** → **কাজ-খ (group work / জোড়ায় কাজ)** → আলোচনা (discussion) → মূল্যায়ন. Protect the individual-then-group order; the individual step is where independent thinking happens.
2. **Map-led / locate-it** — students find a district, river, neighbour-country, continent, or historic site on a map (COUNTRY, GEOGRAPHY, HISTSITES, INTERORG).
3. **Sorting / classify-led** — students sort products (কৃষিজাত vs শিল্পজাত), professions, resources, or rights-vs-duties cards.
4. **Scenario / case-led** — present a short moral, rights, or coexistence scenario; students judge and justify (ETHICS, CITIZEN, COEXIST — the Evaluate reach).
5. **Question-led (কী / কেন / কীভাবে)** — open question before content; students hypothesise (the free-thinking pattern, all strands).
6. **Paired/group exploration (জোড়ায় / দলগত কাজ)** — discuss before whole-class share (the NCF collaborative manner).
7. **Timeline / sequence-led** — order national-history events (LANGMOV → LIBERATION) or a money/budget sequence.
8. **Do-it / role-rehearsal as oneself** — practise a disaster-response drill, a road-safety crossing, a budget calculation **as oneself**, not impersonating a character (C-19 boundary).
9. **Own-opinion prompt (নিজের মতামত / যুক্তি দেখাও)** — each student gives and defends a view on a rights/ethics/environment question (the free-thinking flagship for BGS).
10. **Local-investigation (অনুসন্ধান)** — students gather a fact from home/community (a local occupation, a family budget item, a local historic place) and report — self-discovery extended beyond the classroom.

### 3.4 Free thinking within an Islamic framing — the PROTECTED / mission-critical posture

### 3.5 Redirection patterns (the discovery-stays-Islamic move)
When a student's hypothesis, opinion, or example diverges from Islamic values, the teacher redirects **without shaming**:
- *Student frames a festival/dance as something "we celebrate"* → acknowledge the cultural knowledge, redirect: "এটি অন্যদের সংস্কৃতি — আমরা জেনে রাখি, শ্রদ্ধা করি" — factual knowledge without participation framing (C-03/C-04).
- *Student treats a national leader / monument with reverence (শ্রদ্ধা as worship-adjacent)* → keep the historical respect, re-anchor: "আমরা ইতিহাস জানি ও সম্মান করি; ইবাদত কেবল আল্লাহর জন্য" (C-18).
- *Student personifies nature / treats a resource as self-existing* → "এই সম্পদ কে দিয়েছেন? এটি আমাদের কাছে আমানত" — re-anchor in Allah and stewardship (C-09).
- *Student names a Western figure / brand as an example* → "আমাদের চেনা, আমাদের দেশের কাউকে বলি তো" — redirect to a local/Muslim example (C-10/F-01).
The redirection preserves the child's initiative (the free-thinking value) and corrects only the framing. These patterns seed Project 07 teacher training.
