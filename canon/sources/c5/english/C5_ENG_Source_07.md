# Unit 7 — The Sundarbans
**ছাপা পৃষ্ঠা ৩৫–৩৮**

## পাঠ্যাংশ (verbatim)

### 1.1 Look at the pictures. Ask and answer the following questions in pairs.

*(পৃষ্ঠা ৩৫ · তিনটি ছবি, নিচে চিহ্নিত a · b · c — a: নদীর পাশে ম্যানগ্রোভের শ্বাসমূল · b: বনে দাঁড়ানো বাঘ · c: গাছের নিচে চিত্রা হরিণ)*

> a) What do you see in the pictures?
> b) Where do these animals live?
> c) Have you ever visited a forest?

### 1.2 Listen and read. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৩৫–৩৬)*

*(লেখার সঙ্গে তিনটি ছবি — সুন্দরবনের তীর, ঘাসের মধ্যে বাঘ, আর জলের ধারে বন।)*

> The Sundarbans is a large forest of mangrove trees in Bangladesh. It protects our country in many ways. The strong roots of the mangrove trees save the land from the big waves. The roots keep our lands safe from coastal erosion. The Sundarbans is in the southwestern part of Bangladesh. It is located in Khulna, Satkhira and Bagerhat districts. It was declared a World Heritage Site by the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) in 1997.
>
> The Sundarbans provides a large number of wildlife. The Royal Bengal Tiger, spotted deer, saltwater-crocodile, *Irrawaddy* dolphins and various types of beautiful birds are the main attractions of this forest. People visit here from home and abroad to enjoy the natural beauties and the wildlife. The Sundarbans helps us in many ways. It guards our land from tsunamis, cyclones, and tidal waves. The Sundarbans is a habitat for many endangered animals. It is also a good source of palm leaves, wood and honey. Local people collect these resources for their livelihood. To keep our country safe from natural disasters, we should protect the Sundarbans.

*(প্রথম অনুচ্ছেদ পৃষ্ঠা ৩৫-এ “It is located” পর্যন্ত; বাকিটা পৃষ্ঠা ৩৬-এ।)*

## অনুশীলনী (verbatim)

### 2.1 Read the text again and answer the following questions. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৩৭)*

> a) Where is the Sundarbans located?
> b) What kind of trees make up the Sundarbans forest?
> c) What animals are found in the Sundarbans?
> d) What do people collect from the Sundarbans for their livelihood?
> e) Why is it important to protect the Sundarbans?

### 3.1 Rearrange the following sentences to make a meaningful story. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৩৭)*

> a) It was a stormy night and it rained heavily.
> b) However, the Sundarbans protected him from the cyclone.
> c) Suruj Mian lived in a small village near the Sundarbans.
> d) He thought that his house, cattles and everything would be washed away.
> e) He was very scared on the night of the Cyclone ‘Remal’.

*(নিচে লেখার জন্য দাগ টানা একটি ফাঁকা ঘর।)*

### 4.1 Work in groups. Discuss and make a list of how the Sundarbans help us. Present your list to the class. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৩৮)*

*(নিচে একটি খাতার পাতার ছবি; তার উপরে হাতে-লেখা ধাঁচের হরফে শিরোনাম, বাকিটা ফাঁকা।)*
