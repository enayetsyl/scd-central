# Unit 1 — At the Library
**ছাপা পৃষ্ঠা ১–৬**

## পাঠ্যাংশ (verbatim)

### 1.1 Look at the pictures. In pairs, ask and answer the following questions.

*(পৃষ্ঠা ১ · দুটি ছবি: “PUBLIC LIBRARY” ফটক · লাইব্রেরির ভেতরে তিনজন)*

> a) Have you ever gone to a library?
> b) What do you do in the library?
> c) What kind of books do you like?

### 1.2 Read and act out.

*(পৃষ্ঠা ১–২ · পৃষ্ঠা ২-তে একটি ছবি: বাড়ি ফেরার পথে তিনজন)*

> Rina and Omar are in the public library. They have come to the library to read books. Today, they have seen a new girl in the library. She is looking for some books on the bookshelves. On their way back home, they become introduced and have a conversation.

> Rina: Hello! My name is Rina. Are you new here?
> Rupa: Hi! I'm Rupa. My family has just moved to this neighbourhood.
> Omar: Hi Rupa! I'm Omar. It seems you like reading books.
> Rupa: Yes, I love reading books.
> Rina: What kind of books do you like to read?
> Rupa: I love to read storybooks. What about you, Omar?
> Omar: I enjoy reading biographies of famous people.
> Rupa: That sounds wonderful! Would you like to share your choice, Rina?
> Rina: Sure! I find science fiction very interesting.
> Rupa: Yes, I'm also fond of reading science fiction.
> Rina: Have you borrowed any books today?
> Rupa: No, I couldn't. I am waiting for the library card.
> Rina: It is nice meeting you, Rupa.
> Rupa: Pleased to meet you, too. Will you come to the library tomorrow?
> Omar: Yes, hopefully after school we will be here.
> Rina: Great! See you tomorrow, Rupa and Omar.

*(সংলাপ পৃষ্ঠা ১-এ “Rina: What kind of books do you like to read?” পর্যন্ত; বাকিটা পৃষ্ঠা ২-এ।)*

## অনুশীলনী (verbatim)

### 2.1 Pairwork. Ask and answer the questions. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৩)*

> a) Where are Rina and Omar?
> b) Why do they go there?
> c) Who is the new person there?
> d) What kinds of books Rina and Omar like?
> e) What is Rupa's choice about reading book?

### 3.1 Language Focus: Stressed Syllables *(পৃষ্ঠা ৩)*

> Look at the words used in the dialogue:
> *neighbourhood, tomorrow, enjoy, famous, biographies, fiction, wonderful, hopefully*
> Some syllables are stressed when you say the words. The stressed syllables are marked in capital letters below. Now, say the words aloud.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| en-JOY | FIC-tion | FA-mous |
| WON-der-ful | HOPE-ful-ly | to-MORR-ow |
| bi-o-GRAPH-ies | NEIGH-bour-hood | |

### 3.1.1 Clap your hands when you say the stressed syllables. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৩)*

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| APP-le | TEA-cher | BIRTH-day | PIC-nic |
| po-TA-too | ba-NA-na | re-MEM-ber | com-PU-ter |
| to-DAY | thir-TEEN | THIR-ty | de-CIDE |

### 3.2 Language Focus: Stressed Words *(পৃষ্ঠা ৪)*

> When we speak, we often say some words in a sentence a little louder or with more emphasis. These are **stressed words**. They help us understand the part of the sentence emphasised.
> Now, read these sentences from the story. The **stressed words** are marked in bold.

| Stressed words in sentences | The bold word... |
|---|---|
| My family has **just** moved to this neighbourhood. | tells time of something happened |
| I love to read **storybooks**. | tells what kind of books |
| What **kind** of books do you like? | asks about your choice |
| I find **science fictions** very interesting. | tells favourite kind of book |
| Will you come to the library **tomorrow**? | asks about when |

### 3.2.1 Read the sentences below using stress on the bold words: *(পৃষ্ঠা ৪)*

> a) Do you like **ice-cream**?
> b) We play **football** every day.
> c) She has a **red** bicycle.
> d) Are you **ready** for the test?
> e) I study in class **five**.

### 3.2.2 Language Focus: Going up and down of our Voices *(পৃষ্ঠা ৫)*

> Our voices go up (↗) or down (↘) when we talk. Look at the following sentences used in the conversation.

| বাঁ ঘর | ডান ঘর |
|---|---|
| • Hello! <br> • Are you new here? <br> • Have you borrowed any book? <br> • Will you come to the library tomorrow? | • Hi <br> • Yes, I am. <br> • No, I couldn't. <br> • Yes, I will |
| In these sentences, our voices go up (↗). Usually, our voices go up for <br> • Greetings <br> • Questions which you can answer with ***yes*** or ***no*** | When we answer these questions, our voices go down (↘). |

> Again, look at the following sentences used in the conversation:
> • What kind of books do you like to read?
> • What about you, Omar?
>
> In these sentences, our voices go down (↘).
> Usually, our voices go down for
> • Statements
> • Questions begin with Who, What, When, Where, Why and How.

*(বইয়ে ↗ ↘ দুটি আঁকা তির-চিহ্ন; এখানে টেক্সটে বোঝানোর জন্য লেখা হলো।)*

## এই ইউনিটে যা নেই

- **শব্দার্থের তালিকা নেই।** ইংরেজি বইয়ে বাংলা বইয়ের “অর্থ জেনে নিই” ধরনের কোনো ছক নেই — এই ইউনিটেও নেই।
