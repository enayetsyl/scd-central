### ছাপা 8 (PDF 9)

My School Assembly

**3.1  Language Focus: Preposition (at, in)**

> Look at the sentence used in the text.
>
>     I study ***at*** <u>Azimia Government Primary School</u> ***in*** <u>Chandpur</u>.
>
> - ‘***at***’ is used before the <u>exact place of something or someone</u>.
>     **Examples:**  He is ***at*** the <u>hospital</u> now.
>     He was ***at*** the <u>market</u> yesterday.
>
> - ‘***at***’ is also used to refer to <u>a particular time</u>.
>     **Examples:**  I get up ***at*** <u>6 a.m.</u>
>     They started ***at*** <u>9 o'clock</u> in the morning.
>
> - ‘***in***’ is used before <u>the name of a place or area</u> to say where someone or something is.
>     **Examples:**  She lives ***in*** <u>Hemayetpur</u>.
>     The children are playing ***in*** the <u>field</u>.
>
> **Note:** In a sentence, we use ‘***at***’ before the name of a small place, and use ‘***in***’ before the name of a large place.
>     **Example:**  She lives ***at*** <u>Fulbaria</u> ***in*** <u>Mymensingh</u>.
>     He is now living ***at*** <u>Kaladeba</u> ***in*** <u>Ramgrah</u> upazila.
