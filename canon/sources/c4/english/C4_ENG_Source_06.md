## Numbers

**5.1  Look at the pictures. Discuss in pairs about the numbers marked on the banknotes.**

[ছবি: four photographs of the reverse of Bangladeshi banknotes, two above and two below. Each has one or two white-outlined rectangles drawn over parts of the note to mark them. The numerals and lettering visible inside the notes are recorded under চিত্র-বাহিত লেখা.]

**5.2  Read the text.**

Many people in our country work in different factories. They work hard for their living. Riad is one of them. He works in a jute factory. 550 people work in the factory. Riad received his first salary in January. He was very excited. He went to a shop to buy something for his family. He bought two exercise books and a pair of shoes for his son. He pays 120 taka for the exercise books and 1000 taka for the shoes. He also bought some fresh fruits with 400 taka.

He always completes his duties with honesty and sincerity. The factory becomes satisfied with his performance and made his job permanent in the twelfth month. This year Riad received the Best Worker Award from the factory for his integrity and hard work. The workers like Riad are the true heroes of our country.

## ⛔ উৎস-সীমা

1. ছাপা 19, activity 5.1: the instruction sends pupils to "the numbers marked on the banknotes", but only three of the four notes carry a marked Latin numeral. On the top-left note the two marked areas contain Bengali script and a vertical strip, neither of which resolves to a number at this scan quality. The task cannot be completed from the fourth picture as printed here.
2. ছাপা 19, activity 5.1: the banknote photographs are low-resolution and washed out, and the white marker rectangles overlap the printing beneath them. Several denominations are legible only from the Bengali corner numerals, not from the marked areas.
3. ছাপা 19, reading text: tense shifts within one paragraph — "He bought two exercise books and a pair of shoes for his son. He pays 120 taka for the exercise books and 1000 taka for the shoes." Past and present are mixed for the same transaction.
4. ছাপা 19, reading text: "The factory becomes satisfied with his performance and made his job permanent in the twelfth month." — present "becomes" coordinated with past "made"; also a factory, not a person, is said to become satisfied.
5. ছাপা 19, reading text: "The workers like Riad are the true heroes of our country." — as printed.
6. ছাপা 19, reading text: the text says Riad received his first salary in January and was made permanent "in the twelfth month", which question 5.3 d) then asks him to name as a month; the text never names December.
7. ছাপা 20, activity 5.3: the heading reads "Discuss the following question in pairs." (singular) above five questions.
8. ছাপা 20, activity 5.3 b): "What is the price of two exercise books?" uses the present tense against a past-tense narrative, matching the text's own slip at ⛔ 3.
9. ছাপা 20, activity 5.5: the item labels are "a." to "f." with full stops, while every other lettered list in this unit and in Units 1–5 uses "a)" with a closing bracket.
10. ছাপা 20, activity 5.4: the grid's first two rows run in unbroken sequence 101–120, but the third row is a selection (215, 333, 465, 582, 694, 727, 844, 970, 999, 1000) with no stated pattern and no heading to mark the change.
11. ছাপা 20, activity 5.6: the three blocks are ordered 31–36, 37–40 with 44 and 55 appended, then 68, 73, 86, 91, 99, 100. The second block breaks its own run after 40 and the third is a selection; no note explains the jumps.
12. ছাপা 21, activity 5.8: the table gives the ordinal figure and the ordinal word in two unlabelled sub-columns under a single "Ordinal numbers" header, so the header spans two different things.
13. ছাপা 21, activity 5.9: the worked example fills only the first cell of the left block; the right block begins at 12 with no example, and the two blocks share no header.
14. ছাপা 21, activity 5.7: the eight numbers to be written out (41, 52, 79, 96, 44, 68, 83, 100) are printed in a two-column arrangement to be read across, not down, but the ruled lines for the answers are of two visibly different lengths between the left and right columns.
15. Unit-level numbering: activities run 1.1, 2.1, 3.1, 4.1, 4.2, then 5.1 to 5.9. Group 5 carries nine members, more than the whole of the rest of the unit; the subordinate heading "Numbers" on ছাপা 19 marks the change of topic but the numbering does not restart.
16. The whole unit is raster only. No printed typographic detail below the resolution of a 200 DPI render — the hyphens in the syllabified words and in the number words, the raised ordinal endings, the Bengali numerals inside the banknote photographs — can be independently confirmed. The banknote images in particular are well below the resolution at which their own printed text could be read.
