**3.1  Language Focus: Telling the Sequence**

> In the conversation, Rajib used the words ***First, Next, Then, After that, Finally*** to describe the sequence of their planned activities at the book fair.
>
> When we talk about a planned activity or event, we use the words **First, Next, Then, After that, Finally** to tell the sequence.
>
> **Example: First**, I wake up early in the morning. **Next**, I brush my teeth. **Then**, I have my breakfast. **After that**, I wear my school dress. **Finally**, I get ready at 8 a.m. for school.

## ⛔ উৎস-সীমা

1. ছাপা 57, activity 1.2: "Are'nt you excited to visit there?" — the apostrophe is printed between the **e** and the **n**, not before the **t**. Re-read at high magnification on the word.
2. ছাপা 57, activity 1.2: the lead-in says "Joseph and his friend's plan" — singular possessive, though four pupils take part in the conversation.
3. ছাপা 57, activity 1.2: Joseph's second turn, "Would you like to join us?", is addressed to Taposi and Biplob, who have not yet entered the conversation and are only named in Rajib's preceding turn. Their replies open the next page with no introduction.
4. ছাপা 59, activity 2.2 e): "Why did Biplob think it would be an enjoyable weekend?" — in the dialogue it is **Taposi** who says "Sure, hope, it will be an enjoyable weekend for us." Biplob's last turn is "See you all on Saturday then." The question misattributes the line.
5. ছাপা 58, activity 1.2: "We'll enjoy a lot." and "Sure, hope, it will be an enjoyable weekend for us." — both as printed, the second with commas around "hope".
6. ছাপা 58, activity 1.2: "You can see and buy new books that you like most." — as printed.
7. ছাপা 59, activity 2.1: the answer space is a single empty rounded box with the worked entry "First," set at its lower left. No rules, numbering or slots indicate that four more words are wanted.
8. ছাপা 60, activity 4.1: "We need to start making  preparation for a successful event." — a double space is printed between "making" and "preparation", and the noun is singular without an article.
9. ছাপা 60, activity 4.1: "we will invite our teachers and other guests to present at the event" — "to present" as printed, where "to be present" would be expected.
10. ছাপা 60, activity 4.1: the word box lists the five connectives out of sequence — "then, next, first, finally, after that" — while the passage requires them in the order first, next, then, after that, finally.
11. ছাপা 60, activity 4.2: "you helped your mother in household chores  last weekend" — a double space is printed between "chores" and "last".
