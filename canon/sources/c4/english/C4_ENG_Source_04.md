**3.1  Language Focus: Short Form of Verbs.**

> In the dialogue, we have used the sentence- ***I'm fine.*** Here ***I'm*** is the short form of ***I am***. More examples are given below —
>
> | | | |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | How is—How's | They are—They're | I will—I'll |
> | He is—He's | You are—You're | We will—We'll |
> | She is—She's | We are—We're | We have—We've |
> | It is—It's | How are—How're | |
>
> We use the short form of verbs when we talk to someone.
