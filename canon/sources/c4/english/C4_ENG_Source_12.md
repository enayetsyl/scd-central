### ছাপা 52 (PDF 53)

Making Friends

**1.3  Listen and read the dialogue.**

Rubel and Rajib are in the playground. They found a new boy sitting at a corner. They wanted to take him in their team and make him their friend.

    Rubel    : Rajib, have you noticed the boy sitting alone there?

    Rajib    : Yes, let's go and talk to him.

    Rubel    : Hello! I'm Rubel. What's your name?

    Biplob   : I'm Biplob. We have recently moved to this area.

    Rajib    : Nice to meet you, Biplob. I'm Rajib.

    Biplop   : Nice to meet you all.

    Rubel    : We are playing football. Would you like to join us?

    Biplob   : Oh, I'm not very good at football.

    Rajib    : Don't worry, Biplob. We can show you some tricks.

    Biplob   : Well, that sounds great!

    Rubel    : We'll be playing a friendly match tomorrow.

    Rajib    : I'm sure you'll enjoy.

    Biplob   : Thank you. I hope it'll be a great fun. Thanks for being my
               friend.

    Rajib    : You are welcome. See you tomorrow.

## ⛔ উৎস-সীমা

1. ছাপা 52, activity 1.3: the sixth speaker label is printed **Biplop**, where the same character is **Biplob** in his other four turns and in the lead-in paragraph. A single-letter variant of one speaker's name inside one dialogue.
2. ছাপা 54, activity 3.1: the heading is "Language Focus: **Forms of Request**", but the box contains greetings, introductions, getting-to-know questions, plan-making, agreeing, polite disagreement, positivity and closings. No item in it is a request, and the unit teaches none. The request forms are in Unit 5 (ছাপা 16, "Use of Can/Could for Request").
3. ছাপা 55: the page carries no activity number, no heading and no running instruction — only three boxed tables continuing 3.1 from the previous page. It is the only page in the book so far with no numbered content of its own.
4. ছাপা 55: "Thanks for joining us." is printed twice, once under **Showing Positivity** and once under **Ending Conversations**, as the first item of each.
5. ছাপা 55, **Ending Conversations**: "See you later." and "See you tomorrow." are printed in a single cell with no rule between them, while every other item in the three boxes has its own ruled cell.
6. ছাপা 54, activity 3.1: "These are–" ends with a short dash rather than a colon; the glyph is a short dash and the exact character cannot be determined from the raster.
7. ছাপা 54, activity 3.1, **Suggesting**: "We could ............... ." ends with a spaced full stop while its two siblings end with question marks, and "Let's   ...............?" is printed with a three-space gap after the apostrophe.
8. ছাপা 53, activity 2.1: the box lists "Thanks for being my friend." before "Thank you.", reversing the order in which Biplob says them in the 1.3 dialogue, where "Thank you." comes first.
9. ছাপা 53, activity 2.1: the closing sentence reads "…showed politeness to Biplob and made him their friends." — plural "friends" for a single person.
10. ছাপা 53, activity 2.2: the worked answer for "Greet and reply" is "Hi! / Hello!", but neither *Hi!* nor a reply pair occurs in the 1.3 dialogue, which opens "Rajib, have you noticed…" and greets with "Hello!" only. The answer is taken from the 1.1 speech bubbles on ছাপা 51 instead.
11. ছাপা 53, activity 2.2: category names are capitalised inconsistently — "Greet and reply", "Getting to know each other", "Making Plans", "Agree or accept", "Closing".
12. ছাপা 52, activity 1.3: "I'm sure you'll enjoy." — no object, as printed.
13. ছাপা 52, activity 1.3: "I hope it'll be a great fun." — as printed.
