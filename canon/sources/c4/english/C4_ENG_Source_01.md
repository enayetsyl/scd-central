## ⛔ উৎস-সীমা

1. Dot-leader blanks are printed at several different lengths and in two different characters — runs of full stops in 2.2 and 4.1 of ছাপা 2, and ellipsis characters in 4.2 of ছাপা 3. The blanks are reproduced above as dot runs, but the exact dot count per blank is not established from the raster and should be re-checked against the physical book.
2. ছাপা 1, activity 1.1: the Teacher's fifth turn runs to a second line, "Welcome to your new class.", which is printed with no speaker label and indented deeper than the other continuation-free turns. Whether this is a second sentence of the same turn or a lost label cannot be settled from the page.
3. ছাপা 2, activity 2.2, "My friend" box: each line ends with a space before the full stop — "Now, meet my friend ......... ." — while the parallel "Me" box places the full stop immediately after the parenthetical. Inconsistent spacing as printed.
4. ছাপা 2, activity 2.2: the "Me" box cues each blank with a parenthetical ((name), (hobby), (place)); the "My friend" box gives no such cues. Asymmetric as printed.
5. ছাপা 3, activity 3.1 table: the "Time" column mixes registers — "in the morning" and "after sunset" are descriptive, "after 2 p.m." is a clock time. Printed as shown.
6. ছাপা 3, activity 3.1 table: the blank in each expression is printed as five dots preceded by a space, "….. (name)", a shorter and differently-set leader than any other blank in the unit.
7. ছাপা 3, activity 4.2: the "About me" block cues nothing after "I am" and "I like", unlike the parallel 2.2 box on the previous page; the "About my friend" block gives one cued line followed by two wholly blank ruled lines.
8. Running-head inconsistency across the unit: ছাপা 1 carries no running head, ছাপা 2 and ছাপা 3 both carry the unit title. Elsewhere in the book the recto position that ছাপা 3 occupies carries "English for Today" instead (compare ছাপা 5 and ছাপা 7). ছাপা 3 therefore departs from the pattern used from Unit 2 onward.
9. Section numbering runs 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 2.1, 2.2, 3.1, 4.1, 4.2 — group 3 has a single member where groups 1, 2 and 4 have two or three. No number is skipped within a group; recorded here only because the grouping is uneven.
10. The whole unit is raster only. No printed typographic detail below the resolution of a 200 DPI render — hairline punctuation, hyphen versus dash, apostrophe shape — can be independently confirmed.
