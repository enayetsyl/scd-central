## ⛔ উৎস-সীমা

1. ছাপা 5, activity 3.1: the box quotes the closing expression as “Wish you a good day”, but the song it refers back to prints "I wish you a good day." on ছাপা 4. The quoted form does not match the source line.
2. ছাপা 5, activity 3.1: the lead-in ends "…other ways to say" followed by a short dash rather than a colon. The glyph is a short horizontal dash, not a hyphen and not a long dash; the exact character cannot be determined from the raster.
3. ছাপা 5: the 3.1 box lists five expressions; the 4.1 box beside the song lists four, dropping "Have a good day." The two lists disagree, and the reader is sent to "the box on the right" without being told it is the shorter list.
4. ছাপা 5, activity 4.1: the song is reprinted with its last line replaced by "Have a great day.", set in bold in the rounded display face while the four lines above it stay in the body face. The replacement is printed as fact rather than left blank for the pupil.
5. ছাপা 4, activity 2.1 and ছাপা 5, activity 4.1: the same song is set with different line spacing on the two pages; line breaks are identical and have been preserved.
6. Section numbering runs 1.1, 2.1, 3.1, 4.1 — every group has exactly one member, so no activity in this unit has a sibling. Recorded because it differs from the grouping in Units 1 and 3.
7. ছাপা 4 carries no running head; ছাপা 5 carries "English for Today" rather than the unit title. Compare Unit 1, where the equivalent page carries the unit title instead (see Unit 1, ⛔ 8).
8. The whole unit is raster only. No printed typographic detail below the resolution of a 200 DPI render — the shape of the quotation marks around “Wish you a good day”, the dash glyph, apostrophe forms — can be independently confirmed.
