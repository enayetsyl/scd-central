**3.1  Language Focus: Uses of *a.m.* and *p.m.***

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ছবি: a red-rimmed wall clock, hour hand at 9, minute hand at 12] | 9.00 a.m. | nine o'clock in the morning |
| [ছবি: a red-rimmed wall clock, minute hand at 12, hour hand toward the 1–2 position] | 1.00 p.m. | one o'clock in the afternoon |
| [ছবি: a red-rimmed wall clock, one black hand pointing down between 6 and 7] | 6.30 p.m. | half past six in the evening |
| [ছবি: a red-rimmed wall clock, hour hand at 10, minute hand at 12] | 10.00 p.m. | ten o'clock at night |

We use ***a.m.*** (ante meridiem) to show that the time between midnight (12 o'clock at night) and noon (up to 11:59 at midday).

We use ***p.m.*** (post meridiem) to show that the time between noon (12 o'clock at noon) and midnight (up to 11:59 at night).

## ⛔ উৎস-সীমা

1. ছাপা 24, activity 2.1 b): "When did Jerin attend the Art and Craft class?" — in the 1.2 dialogue it is **Mitu**, not Jerin, who says "I had Art and Craft class at 10.00 a.m." The question cannot be answered from the conversation it points to.
2. ছাপা 23, activity 1.2: "The  programme will end by 12.30 p.m." — a double space is printed between "The" and "programme".
3. ছাপা 23, activity 1.2: "I will join with a small group of students." — "join with" as printed.
4. Time formats are inconsistent across the unit. The dialogue and 3.1 use a full stop (9.00 a.m., 12.30 p.m.); the prose under the 3.1 table uses a colon (11:59); 3.2 uses a colon (9:30 a.m., 4:00 p.m.); 4.1 returns to a full stop (at 6.00 a.m.). All four styles appear within three pages.
5. ছাপা 24, activity 3.1: both explanatory sentences are ungrammatical as printed — "We use ***a.m.*** … to show that the time between midnight … and noon" and the parallel sentence for p.m. A verb is missing after "that the time".
6. ছাপা 24, activity 3.1: the second clock is captioned 1.00 p.m., but its hour hand appears to point closer to the 2 than to the 1. The drawing is small and the hand is thick; this should be checked against the physical book before anything is concluded.
7. ছাপা 24, activity 3.1: on the third clock, captioned 6.30 p.m., only one black hand is clearly resolvable, pointing down between 6 and 7, together with a thin red hand at 12. Whether both hour and minute hands are drawn, and where, cannot be settled from this scan.
8. ছাপা 25, activity 3.2: the heading is "Tell the time." but all three rows are already filled in — clock, words and figures. There is nothing for the pupil to complete, unlike 4.2 on the next page which sets the same task with blanks.
9. ছাপা 25, activity 4.1: the worked answer is "at 6.00 a.m." with the preposition included; the instruction asks only for *a.m.* or *p.m.*, so the model answer sets a wider pattern than the task states.
10. ছাপা 26, activity 4.2: the worked answer "half past ten" carries no a.m./p.m. and no "in the morning" phrase, though 3.1, 3.2 and 4.1 all attach one. The six clocks are drawn small and their hand positions are not reliably readable at this resolution; no times have been inferred for the five blank cells.
11. ছাপা 26, activity 4.3: "Look at the digital clock." (singular) followed by "Tell the time of the clocks." (plural), above two clocks.
12. ছাপা 26, activity 4.3: the two displays include an unlit ghost digit at the left of each, an artefact of the seven-segment graphic rather than part of the time. A pupil reading the left clock sees `8` in pale grey before `7:00`.
13. Section numbering runs 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 3.1, 3.2, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 — group 2 has a single member. No number is skipped within a group.
14. The whole unit is raster only. No printed typographic detail below the resolution of a 200 DPI render — the clock hand positions, the maker's lettering on the dials, the full stop versus colon in each printed time — can be independently confirmed.
