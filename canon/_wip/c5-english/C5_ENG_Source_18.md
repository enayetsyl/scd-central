# Unit 18 — Nakshi Kantha
**ছাপা পৃষ্ঠা ৯৭–৯৯**

## পাঠ্যাংশ (verbatim)

### 1.1 Look up the words of column A in the dictionary. Then, match the words with their meanings in column B. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৯৭)*

*(ছকের উপরে একটি ছবি — “DICTIONARY” লেখা একটি বই)*

| Column A | Column B |
|---|---|
| a)  stitch | i)  a way of doing something that has existed for a long time |
| b)  quilt | ii)  to sew pieces of cloth |
| c)  wrap | iii) a warm blanket made by sewing pieces of clothes together |
| d)  pattern | iv) to put cloth over something to cover it |
| e)  tradition | v)  a design that repeats shapes, colours, lines etc |

### 1.2 Read the following passage.

*(পৃষ্ঠা ৯৭–৯৮ · পৃষ্ঠা ৯৭-এ একটি ছবি — এক বৃদ্ধা নকশি কাঁথায় সেলাই করছেন; পৃষ্ঠা ৯৮-এ গোল ছবিতে কাঁথার উপর দুটি হাত)*

> When Adiba was in class five, she moved to New York with her parents. Her grandmother lives in a village in Bangladesh. She is very fond of her grandmother. Adiba used to visit her grandmother once every year.

> Last year, her grandmother wanted to give her something on her birthday. So, she decided to sew a special *Nakshi Kantha* for her.

> It is a traditional handmade quilt. She began by gathering colourful pieces of old *sarees*. With great care, she stitched them together. She made a beautiful pattern of flowers and birds on it. It took many days and nights to complete the *Kantha*. Before Adiba's birthday, her grandmother sent her the *Nakshi Kantha*. Adiba was filled with joy after receiving it. Adiba heard from her father that the stitch, colour and pattern of the *Kantha* had a story to tell. From that day, Adiba wanted to learn more about the *Nakshi Kantha*. It was more than just a quilt.

> It was a symbol of her grandmother's love. She felt happy when she wrapped herself with the quilt. It symbolises the rich tradition of her homeland.

*(পড়ার অংশটি পৃষ্ঠা ৯৭-এ “So, she” পর্যন্ত; বাকিটা পৃষ্ঠা ৯৮-এ। বইয়ে *Nakshi Kantha*, *Kantha* ও *sarees* তির্যক হরফে ছাপা।)*

## অনুশীলনী (verbatim)

### 2.1 Read the passage again and answer the following questions. *(পৃষ্ঠা ৯৮)*

> a) What did Adiba's grandmother gather for sewing the *Kantha*?
> b) What patterns did she make on the *Kantha*?
> c) How long did it take to complete the *Kantha*?
> d) How did the *Kantha* tell a story?
> e) How did Adiba feel when she wrapped herself in the quilt?

### 3.1 Language Focus: Simple Past Tense *(পৃষ্ঠা ৯৯)*

*(পুরোটা একটি হলুদ বাক্সে; ভেতরে পাশাপাশি দুটি ছক, বাঁ পাশে খাড়া করে লেখা “Regular Verbs” ও “Irregular Verbs”)*

> In English, **-d**/**-ed** is added to most verbs to tell/show what happened in the past. They are **regular verbs**.
> But **irregular verbs** do not follow the usual rules for changing tenses.

| Regular Verbs — Present | Past |
|---|---|
| live | lived |
| visit | visited |
| decide | decided |
| stitch | stitched |
| fill | filled |
| wrap | wrapped |
| cherish | cherished |

| Irregular Verbs — Present | Past |
|---|---|
| begin | began |
| make | made |
| take | took |
| tell | told |
| feel | felt |
| have | had |
| is/am | was |
