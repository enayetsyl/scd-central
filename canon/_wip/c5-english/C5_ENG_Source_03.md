# Unit 3 — Be Quiet, Please
**ছাপা পৃষ্ঠা ১২–১৫**

## পাঠ্যাংশ (verbatim)

### 1.1 Read the following conversation and act it out. *(পৃষ্ঠা ১২)*

*(একটি ছবি: খোলা দরজার দুপাশে দুই কিশোর — একজন ঠোঁটে আঙুল রেখে চুপ থাকার ইশারা করছে।)*

> [Erhan has come to play at Rayan's home. Rayan's little sister is sleeping in the next room.]

> Erhan: [Loudly] Hello, Rayan, are you there?
> Rayan: Erhan, be quiet, please! My little sister is sleeping.
> Erhan: Oh, sorry. I didn't mean to be loud. Let's play carrom.
> Rayan: Okay, but speak in a low voice. Don't be noisy.
> Erhan: Got it. Give me a glass of water, please. I'm thirsty.
> Rayan: Sure. (Rayan leaves and returns with a glass of water). Here you go. Drink slowly.
> Erhan: Thanks. The water is a bit cold. I prefer warm water.
> Rayan: Me too. Now, get the carrom board. It's near the bookshelf, not that far.
> Erhan: I can't see it. It's dark here.
> Rayan: Oh, I see. Let me turn the light on.
> Erhan: Found it. Oh no, the board looks dirty.
> Rayan: Let's clean it.
> Erhan: Done. Let's play. You go first.
> Rayan: Thanks. But remember, don't talk loudly.
> Erhan: Okay, I'll be quiet.

*(সংলাপ পৃষ্ঠা ১২-এ “Rayan: Me too. Now, get the carrom board. It's near the bookshelf, not that far.” পর্যন্ত; বাকিটা পৃষ্ঠা ১৩-এ।)*

### 3.1 Language Focus: Imperative Sentences *(পৃষ্ঠা ১৪)*

> Read the following sentences used in the text:
> a) Speak in a low voice.
> b) Give me a glass of water.
> c) Don't be noisy.
> d) Be quiet, please.
> e) Let's play carrom.
>
> These sentences tell someone to do something. These are imperative sentences.
> An imperative sentence gives a command, instruction or express request. It usually starts with a verb.
> For example,

| | |
|---|---|
| Be kind. <br> Listen carefully. <br> Open your book. <br> Wash your hands. <br> Please, keep silent. | Don't touch that! <br> Don't make a noise. |
| | Let me recite the poem. <br> Let her explain the topic. <br> Let us play together. |

*(বইয়ে ডান দিকের দুটি ঘর আলাদা দুটি বাক্স, বাঁ দিকে একটি লম্বা বাক্স।)*

### 4.1 Write imperative sentence for each of the following statements. The first one is done for you. *(পৃষ্ঠা ১৫)*

> a) Your friend is making a noise while you are doing your homework.
> Imperative sentence: Be quiet, please.
>
> b) You are asking your classmate to help carry your bag.
> Imperative sentence: ______ .
>
> c) You want someone to clean the board.
> Imperative sentence: ______ .
>
> d) You want to play cricket with your friend.
> Imperative sentence: ______ .
>
> e) You want your brother not to go out in the rain.

## এই ইউনিটে যা নেই

- **শব্দার্থের তালিকা নেই।** ইংরেজি বইয়ে বাংলা বইয়ের “অর্থ জেনে নিই” ধরনের কোনো ছক নেই — এই ইউনিটেও নেই।
- **সংলাপ ছাড়া আলাদা পড়ার অনুচ্ছেদ নেই।** ১.১-এর পরিচায়ক এক বাক্য আর সংলাপ — এটুকুই পাঠ্যাংশ।
- **বইয়ের ভেতরে কোনো প্রশ্নের উত্তর দেওয়ার অনুশীলনী নেই।** Unit 2-এর মতো “Answer the questions” ধরনের কাজ এই ইউনিটে নেই; পুরো অনুশীলনী শব্দ ও বাক্যগঠনের।
