# IMAGE_RULES.md — canon

The school's image doctrine. Derived entirely from `canon/islamic-curation/REF-1_Curation_Policy.md`
(v1.2 LOCKED). Every rule below carries its REF-1 reference; cite this file or the C-code, and
do not copy either into a workstream file (AGENTS.md §8).

---

## 1. The order of preference

1. **No living-being image at all.** Carry the learning outcome with objects, scenery, letters
   or diagrams. This is the first preference, not a fallback.
2. **Only where a depiction is genuinely necessary** for the outcome: a figure **without facial
   features** — no eyes, no nose, no ears, no mouth — for humans and animals alike.
3. There is no third option.

REF-1 §4.1 **C-05** (Principal direction, README §14 Q1). C-05's default stance is REPLACE.
The intra-Salafi difference on photographs vs hand-drawings is settled — faceless figures,
avoid where possible — and is not reopened.

## 2. C-05 is applied last

C-05 is a **global rendering constraint applied after** every other category's content edit.
Finish the content work, then pass the image through the faceless rule.

REF-1 **C-05** *Exceptions / edge cases*; REF-1 §2 (teacher workflow) step 6.

## 3. What C-05 does not touch

Inanimate objects, plants, scenery, and non-figurative diagrams are unaffected. A merely
*talking* animal in a fable is a content question, not an image question.

REF-1 **C-05**, **C-09**, §4.5 **E-09b**.

## 4. Grouping — who may appear in one frame

Depict only **mahram groupings** (parents with children, siblings) and **same-gender groups**.
A mixed-gender scene is re-illustrated as a same-gender group or as a family/mahram scene, with
roles and dialogue redistributed so the vocabulary and sentence-pattern outcome is unchanged.
Any retained figure still passes the C-05 faceless rule.

REF-1 §4.1 **C-02**; tagging example in §3 (FLEXIBLE).

## 5. Images that carry nationalist veneration

Flag salutes, flowers or wreaths at the Shaheed Minar or other monuments, and ritual silence
are removed. Hero-portrait counting items are swapped for neutral objects.

REF-1 §4.1 **C-18**, §4.5 **E-18c**.
⚠️ REF-1 §5 **OPEN-02** carries an unresolved caveat on borderline *physical-act* distinctions
under C-18, awaiting qualified-scholar confirmation. The broad C-18 stance is fully in force.

## 6. An image can trigger more than one rule

A sung rhyme illustrated with children is C-03 **and** C-05. Tag every category the element
triggers; do not stop at the first.

REF-1 §2 step 2.

## 7. Scope

REF-1 v1.2 governs **Class 1 Bangla and English only**; other subjects and Classes 2–5 expand
at REF-1 v2.0. Sections 1–6 are canon within that scope. Applying them beyond it is not yet
authorised — see **PENDING-P-001** in root `PENDING_PRINCIPAL.md`.

## 8. What this file deliberately does not cover

This file is **REF-1-derived only** (Principal ruling, CD-007). The following are **out of
scope** and are not school canon: the programmatic white stripe, largest-being-per-frame
selection, any family-scene carve-out beyond §4 above, photocopy-safe fields, and silhouette
treatment for historical figures. Those belong to the storybook venture. **No rule from that
set may be applied to school output by citing this file.** If any of them is ever needed here,
it enters through a new CD row, not by assumption.
