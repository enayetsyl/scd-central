## Why this starts at 016 and not at 001, and not at 007

**A fresh series at 001 would orphan every attested row.** Twelve `D-PROJ01-###` numbers are cited
in this repo today, by REF-04, REF-19 and the four Project-00 registers. Restarting at 001 would
mint new rows that collide with citations already in canon — the D-051 reconstruction problem,
created deliberately rather than inherited.

**The ruling's first form said `-007`**, on the ground that REF-04 attests `-005` and `-006`. That
was correct about REF-04 and wrong about the repo: **a census of every `.md` and `.py` outside
`.git` finds attestation through `-015`.** Starting at 007 would have orphaned nine rows — the same
failure the instruction existed to prevent, one order of magnitude down. Re-ruled to **016**
(Principal, 2026-08-14, continuation ruling 3): *"Same principle, correct data."*

**Nothing here is reconstructed content.** No row below states what its decision *was*. Inventing a
decision body to fill a numbered slot would be worse than an empty slot, because the next session
would cite it.

## What happens if the original register turns up

**It is imported read-only and cited, never continued** — the CD-034 / CD-043(b) pattern. Its rows
replace the RECONSTRUCTED-UNKNOWN states above with real content; **the numbering does not move**,
because live rows may already sit above `-015` by then. If the original turns out to run past `-015`,
**that is a collision and it is the Principal's**, not something a later session resolves by
renumbering — and it is the case AGENTS.md §12.3 covers: report, do not move.
