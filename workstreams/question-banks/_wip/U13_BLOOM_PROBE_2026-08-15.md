# PROBE — four C5 BAN পাঠ ১৩ items tagged on COGNITIVE DEMAND, not on slot identity

**Status: PROBE. Not a wave. Not authored into any bank. Nothing here is promotable.**
**Purpose:** the agent's wave-3 projection derived `bloom_level` from **slot identity**. The
Principal ruled that wrong on 2026-08-15: **Bloom is a property of the item's cognitive demand.**
MarkLogic §৩ assigns **domains to a PAPER**; `QUESTION_POLICY` §3 row 10 maps domain↔Bloom.
**Neither fixes a slot's level.** These four items test the claim before a target is chosen.

**The spine says so in its own words, and the agent had read past it.** `MarkLogic_BAN_Spine.md`,
`BAN-S05`: **"বহুনির্বাচনি হলো *উত্তর দেওয়ার একটা পদ্ধতি*, কোনো দক্ষতা নয়।"** A way of answering,
not a skill. A slot that is explicitly *not a skill* cannot carry a cognitive level.

**This is `CR-007`'s shape.** There, the MATH spine's eleven slots — **পরীক্ষার প্রশ্ন-ধরন**, not
অধ্যায়-বিষয় — were read as chapter subjects, and the whole list shifted one cell. Here the same
spine's slots were read as carrying a Bloom level. **Same error: a spine slot read as carrying a
property it does not carry.**

Source for every stimulus: `canon/marklogic/C5_Bangla_Source_13-23.md`, পাঠ ১৩ পাখির মতো.

## PROBE 1 — S05 বহুনির্বাচনি · aimed at `Remember`

**প্রশ্ন:** 'কর্ণফুলী' শব্দটির অর্থ কী?
**বিকল্প:** ক. একটি নদীর নাম **✓** · খ. একটি ফুলের নাম · গ. একটি পাখির নাম · ঘ. একটি গ্রামের নাম
**Anchor:** *অর্থ জেনে নিই* — `কর্ণফুলী | একটি নদীর নাম`
**Key provenance:** in the extraction. No `model_note` needed.

**TAGGED `Remember`. Reasoning:** the student retrieves one stored fact and matches it. The
distractors are excluded by that same single fact — no second step, nothing composed. The MCQ
format is doing no cognitive work here; the item would be `Remember` written as a short answer too,
**which is the point: the format did not decide the level, the demand did.**

## PROBE 2 — S05 বহুনির্বাচনি · deliberately aimed at `Understand`

**প্রশ্ন:** 'আমি না হয় পাখিই হব, / পাখির মতো বন্য।' — এখানে কবি 'বন্য' হতে চান বলতে কী বোঝাচ্ছেন?
**বিকল্প:**
 ক. তিনি হিংস্র হতে চান
 খ. তিনি বনের ভিতরে বাস করতে চান
 গ. তিনি নিয়মের বাঁধন ছেড়ে স্বাধীন হতে চান **✓**
 ঘ. তিনি পড়াশোনা একেবারে ছেড়ে দিতে চান
**Anchor:** *"আমি না হয় পাখিই হব, / পাখির মতো বন্য।"*
**Key provenance:** the extraction glosses **বন্য = বুনো** and nothing more. The gloss does not
contain the answer. **Teacher-supplied under CD-136** — declared in this item's `model_note`.

**TAGGED `Understand`. Reasoning, and this is the load-bearing one:** the gloss gives the *literal*
sense; the item asks for the sense the poem builds. The student has to hold three separate places
in the text together — পড়া বনাম ওড়া (stanza 1), শহর ছেড়ে গাঁয়ে (stanza 4), মানুষ হওয়া বনাম পাখি
হওয়া (stanza 5) — and restate the result in their own frame. **That is construction of meaning, not
retrieval.**

**The distractors are what prove the level rather than decorate it.** **ক and খ are the glossed
literal reading** — a student who retrieved *বুনো* and stopped picks one of them. **ঘ is the
surface reading of stanza 1.** So **the `Remember` route lands on a wrong answer**, which is the
only real test of whether an item is above `Remember`.

**ANSWERING THE QUESTION THAT WAS ASKED: the format supports `Understand` from this chapter's
content — but narrowly, and the constraint is worth stating.** The poem is 20 lines and carries
**four** interpretable images: দুধভরা ওই চাঁদের বাটি · বন্য · পাখির মতো ডাকা · শহর বনাম গাঁ. That is
the ceiling on genuine `Understand` items from this chapter **in any slot**, S05 included — perhaps
five or six once মিল-শব্দ and মূলভাব are counted. **Beyond that the "কেন" items collapse back into
`Remember`**, because this poem answers most *why* questions verbatim in the next line: *"পাঠে আমার
মন বসে না / কাঁঠালচাঁপার গন্ধে"* is a stated cause, so an MCQ asking কেন পাঠে মন বসে না is
**retrieval wearing a কেন**. That item would be `Remember` and tagging it `Understand` would be the
same error as tagging by slot, one level down.

## PROBE 3 — S12 যুক্তবর্ণ ও শব্দ গঠন

**প্রশ্ন:** 'কর্ণফুলী' শব্দের যুক্তবর্ণটি ভেঙে লিখি।
**উত্তর:** র্ণ = র + ণ (রেফ + ণ)
**Anchor:** *"সবাই যখন ঘুমিয়ে পড়ে / কর্ণফুলীর কূলটায়"*
**Key provenance:** the extraction glosses কর্ণফুলী as *একটি নদীর নাম* and never decomposes it.
**Teacher-supplied under CD-136** — যুক্তবর্ণ বিভাজন is named in the ruling's admitted list.

**TAGGED `Apply`, NOT `Remember` — and the tag carries a condition.** The spine puts C5 at *মূল
কাঠামো* on a ladder whose C3 rung reads **"পুরোপুরি যোগ্যতা হিসেবে"** — a competency, not a list.
The demand is **executing the রেফ rule on a word the student has not previously decomposed**, which
is procedure-on-a-new-instance. **The condition:** if the class has already decomposed this exact
word, the item degrades to `Remember` for that class. **So the tag is a claim about the item as
authored, and it is falsifiable by the teacher** — which is exactly why it belongs in human review
(REF-09 §9) and not in a gate.

## PROBE 4 — S13 এক কথায় প্রকাশ

**প্রশ্ন:** 'নদীর ধারে যে জায়গা' — কবিতা থেকে শব্দ নিয়ে এক কথায় প্রকাশ করি।
**উত্তর:** কূল
**Anchor:** *"সবাই যখন ঘুমিয়ে পড়ে / কর্ণফুলীর কূলটায়"*
**Key provenance:** কূল appears in the poem and in অনুশীলনী ১'s word list; **the *অর্থ জেনে নিই* table
glosses only four words and কূল is not one of them.** Teacher-supplied under CD-136.

**TAGGED `Remember`, and this one goes the way the slot-derived table guessed — which is worth
recording, because a probe that confirmed nothing would be as useless as one that overturned
everything.** Mapping a definition-phrase to a single word is retrieval of a stored language fact.
The instruction *"কবিতা থেকে শব্দ নিয়ে"* narrows the search to twenty lines; it does not add a step.

## WHAT THE PROBE SETTLES

| Slot | Slot-derived guess | Probe finding | Moved? |
|---|---|---|---|
| S05 বহুনির্বাচনি | `Remember` (mostly) | **carries both** — `Remember` and `Understand`, the latter capped by the poem's four images | **YES** |
| S12 যুক্তবর্ণ | `Remember` | **`Apply`** — rule execution on a new instance, conditional on prior class work | **YES** |
| S13 এক কথায় প্রকাশ | `Remember` | `Remember` | no |
| S04 শূন্যস্থান | `Remember` | `Remember` — a blank on a memorised line is recitation | no |
| S06 বিপরীত শব্দ | `Remember` | `Remember` — recall of a language fact | no |
| S11 প্রশ্ন তৈরি / বিরামচিহ্ন | `Apply` | `Apply` | no |

**Two of six moved. The slot-derived table was not wrong everywhere — it was wrong where the slot's
NAME is a format (`বহুনির্বাচনি`) or a procedure (`যুক্তবর্ণ ভেঙে`), and right where the slot's name
happens to describe a recall task.** That is why the error was hard to see: **it produced correct
answers four times out of six for the wrong reason.**
