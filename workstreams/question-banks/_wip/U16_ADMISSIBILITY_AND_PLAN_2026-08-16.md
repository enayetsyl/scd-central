## 3. Curation — this chapter carries the source's heaviest ⚠ block, and every clause binds

- **"শহিদ" — no definitional item, anywhere.** The source: questions must be about *ব্যক্তিদের
  ঘটনা ও অবদান*, never *"শহিদ কারা"*. **No item in this bank asks what শহিদ means, who counts as
  one, or invites the student to apply the word.** The word appears only where it is part of a
  proper name the book itself prints — **শহিদ সাবের** (a person's name) and **শহিদ বুদ্ধিজীবী
  দিবস** (the day's official name). Elsewhere the bank writes *প্রাণ দেন · হত্যা করা হয় ·
  বুদ্ধিজীবী*.
- **C-03 music.** The chapter's গান ও সুরকার paragraph is barred as a source. It is **not in the
  extraction at all**, so no anchor can reach it; nothing is authored from it.
- **C-18 nationalist veneration.** No item touches শহিদ মিনারে ফুল দেওয়া or any ritual of
  homage. REF-01 §4.1 C-18's own carve-out is what this bank uses: **factual history — dates,
  events, contributions — is taught.**
- **C-05 images.** No item carries or requests a picture of any person.
- **REF-2 names.** No invented personal name appears in any stem, option, key or rubric row; the
  only personal names are the historical figures the chapter itself records, which are content
  facts and not characters (QB-CR-005 is about substituted characters, not about history).
