| QB-D-008 | 2026-08-09 | **`gates.py` hardened after the audit proved it could not fail on a factually wrong bank.** The audit built a corrupt bank with eight defects a teacher would reject — a flipped answer key, a fabricated fact, an MCQ with two correct options, one with none, a cross-pool paraphrase, a missing (স), an invented quotation in straight quotes, and a wrong শব্দার্থ key — and the gate returned **CLEAN**. Root causes and fixes, each now carrying its own seeded selftest case: **(a) SOURCE-TRACE was vacuous** — it only asked whether the anchor appeared anywhere in the chapter, so the two-character word "না" satisfied it; an anchor must now be a 3+ token span, present in the chapter, **and share vocabulary with the item it claims to source**.
