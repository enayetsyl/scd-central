## How to use this glossary

1. This is the single source of truth. If a term is defined here and used differently elsewhere, the elsewhere is wrong.
2. Entries are alphabetical. The topical checklist above is for navigation only — definitions never duplicate.
3. To add a term: append at the bottom of its alphabetical position, log the addition in §"Version log" with date and reason, and (per PROJECT00_README.md §5.4) flag any Project whose Instructions or files need to reflect the new term.
4. To revise a term: replace the definition in place, log the revision in §"Version log", and flag affected Projects.
5. To remove a term: keep the entry with a `DEPRECATED — see [New Term]` note for at least 60 days before deleting, so older chats remain interpretable.

## Entries (alphabetical)

### Annotated Skeleton

The output of applying the Islamic Curation Policy to an NCTB stability skeleton. Every chapter and lesson is tagged with one of three annotations — [KEEP-AS-IS](#keep-as-is), [NEEDS-REPLACEMENT](#needs-replacement), or [FLEXIBLE](#flexible) — so downstream lesson planners and content creators know what to preserve and what to replace. Produced in Project 03. Step 8.1 of the 15-day plan.

### Approved Names Pool

The vetted pool of Bengali-Muslim personal names that downstream Projects substitute in whenever the [Islamic Curation Policy](#islamic-curation-policy) §4.1 C-10 / flag F-01 requires a Western or non-Muslim personal name to be replaced. Lives in Project 00 as **REF-20** (`LOCKED_REF-20_Approved_Names_Pool_v1_0.md`). One pool per class (C1–C5), each split Male / Female; names sourced from the companions given glad tidings of Jannah, the wives & daughters of the Prophet ﷺ and the four khulafa, major hadith-narrators, other major companions and sahabiyyat, and notable scholars (by given name). Male names are distinct across classes; female names may recur. A **living/append-extensible** file (name additions are data-fills, not version supersedes). Direct consumers: Projects 03 (annotation) and 05 (authoring); also Project 04 (question content). See REF-20 §0 for the picking rules.

### Assignment Tracker

Operational tracker for take-home assignments, cadence Thursday and Sunday. One of the 8 trackers in the pack (D-013). Lives in Project 06. Captures: student, subject, assignment ID, issue date, due date, submission status, score, follow-up status.

### Bloom's Taxonomy

The cognitive-domain hierarchy (Remember → Understand → Apply → Analyze → Evaluate → Create) used as the universal framework across this initiative for lesson plan design, question setting, and assessment. The canonical reference document is `Bloom_Taxonomy_Comprehensive_Primer_Teachers_V1A.docx` in Project 00, with V1B (Standard Reference) and V1C (Daily-Use Pocket) derivative versions to be produced later. See also [V1A / V1B / V1C](#v1a--v1b--v1c).

### Borderline Register

The list, compiled in every annotated skeleton, of CASE-BY-CASE / OPEN curation items (mythological proper-nouns, garland/bindi imagery, festival scenes, C-18 borderline civic acts under REF-01 §5 OPEN-02) that the annotator does **not** resolve alone but escalates to the Principal for a ruling **before the skeleton locks**. Required by REF-21 §2; each ruling then feeds back as a REF-01 §4.5 edge case. See [Annotated Skeleton](#annotated-skeleton), [Element-level Scan](#element-level-scan).

### Built Asset

A derived, version-stamped file that carries an **extracted copy** of one or more canonical references' payload, assembled so a chat can load a few sources instead of many — e.g. the Project 03 [Production Core](#production-core) (built from REF-02/07/08/18). Because a built asset duplicates source content it can go **stale**: when a source reference is superseded, the asset must be **rebuilt from the new source** before reuse. The dependency is recorded in `PROJECT00_CROSS_PROJECT_INDEX.md` (the source's consumer rows name the asset), and the rebuild is enforced at change-time by PROJECT00_README.md **§5.3** (pre-supersede dependent check) / **§5.4** (supersede-dependent rule). **Payload scope:** only a change to the *extracted payload* triggers a rebuild — a payload-neutral edit to the source (e.g. a back-pointer footer) does not. Introduced D-PROJ00-061. See [Cross-Project Propagation](#cross-project-propagation), [Production Core](#production-core).

### Category Header (REF-19)

The top-level grouping under which a subject's canonical Topic IDs are filed in the [Vertical Topic Progression Map](#vertical-topic-progression-map). REF-19's hybrid vocabulary works **per domain**: the first subject of a domain seeds shared headers; later subjects of the *same* domain **reuse** them; a subject in a genuinely different domain **self-seeds** its own headers (REF-19 §1.3 / Step-3 "extend only with justification"). As of REF-19 v1.6 there are **two header families** — the six **literacy** headers seeded by Bangla and reused by English (A Sound & Letter · B Word & Vocabulary · C Sentence & Grammar · D Oracy · E Reading & Literary Forms · F Composition & Writing), and the seven **numeracy** headers self-seeded by Math as the first non-literacy subject (N Number & Place Value · O Operations & Mathematical Sentences · T Number Theory & Patterns · R Fractions/Decimals/Percentage · M Measurement & Money · G Geometry · D Data Handling). A future subject reuses whichever family fits its domain or self-seeds a new one (Science is expected to self-seed a science family). See REF-19 §1.3 and [Vertical Topic Progression Map](#vertical-topic-progression-map).

### Chapter Plan

The chapter-scope plan that holds Layer 1 of the [Three-Layer Lesson Plan](#three-layer-lesson-plan) when a chapter spans more than one teaching period. New in REF-02 **v1.3** (master **D-050**, 2026-05-28). Carries the seven Spine fields planned **once at chapter scope** (objective set, must-cover, Bloom progression, exit-check Bank, Islamic-alignment note, Revision Anchor, Homework Specification) **plus the [Session Map](#session-map)** (the former eighth field, Homework Question Pool, is retired — D-051) (§2.9, the chapter→sessions backbone). Authored **first**, at the chapter-planning gate, where the session count is set per REF-02 §2A.3 (TG-Reconciliation baseline; ±40% or ±1 band; logged reason within-band, Subject-Lead approval beyond-band). A **single-period chapter** has no separate Chapter Plan — it collapses to one standalone [Session Plan](#session-plan) that inlines the Spine (REF-02 §2A.2). Filename: `C{class}_{SUBJECT}_U{nn}_ChapterPlan_v{ver}.md` (D-037 ripple, v1.3). Bangla term: usually the book's own division word — পাঠ, অধ্যায়, ইউনিট, etc. (= the [Division Anchor](#division-anchor)).

The original named-ban wording is preserved in the v1.21 version-log row.)* See README §3 D-049.

### Cross-Project Propagation

Standing convention (PROJECT00_README.md §5.4): when a fact, decision, or change in a chat could affect another Project or an initiative-wide standing rule, Claude pauses, states which Project(s) are affected, drafts the exact text needed for that Project's README/DECISIONS, and asks the Principal whether to apply now or add to that Project's TODO.

### Daily Homework Budget

School-level governance figure: the maximum total daily homework time across all subjects for a given class. **Uniform across Classes 1–5: 2–4 hours daily** (school nights Sun–Thu) **plus an 8-hour weekend assignment cap** (4 hrs × Fri+Sat) — Class 1 per D-024, extended to Classes 2–5 by D-036 (Principal direction 2026-05-22). Authoritative figures live in **REF-08 ([Homework Architecture](#homework-architecture)), LOCKED v1.0**. The ceiling is an **average-student design target**, not a hard per-student limit (fast students finish under; weak students may run over with reactive Pool top-ups). The split across subjects is **not fixed** — each lesson's [Homework Specification](#homework-specification) sets that subject's time (Class-1 band 0–40 min, default 20) and the class teacher reconciles the day's sum against the ceiling, trimming by question count not time (D-030). Daily Quran muraja'ah is additional to and outside this budget. The Homework Tracker (Project 06) monitors aggregate daily load per student against the ceiling. See REF-08 §2.

### Discovery Pattern Suggestions

A pre-prepared inventory of discovery-oriented teaching patterns provided per lesson plan. Minimum 3 patterns per lesson per D-032. The teacher's fallback when they want a discovery approach but don't have one in mind. Common pattern types: question-led, demonstration-then-question, paired-exploration, object-led, story-led, comparison-led, sorting-led. Subject-specific canonical pattern libraries live in REF-03+ Subject Spine Playbooks. See REF-02 §4.5.

### Division Anchor

The `U{nn}` token in the content-filename / Pool-ID convention (README §5.2, D-037): the book's **primary numbered division** — chapter (অধ্যায়), unit (Unit), lesson (পাঠ), or dars (الدرس) — captured **by position** from the completed [Stability Analysis](#stability-analysis) skeleton (the single source of truth) and normalized to zero-padded 2-digit Latin digits (U01, U02 …). Named "division anchor" because most NCTB books are single-level and the dividing word/numbering varies by subject and class. `U` is kept for continuity with the Pool-ID format (D-028) but does **not** mean specifically "unit." A second token `_L{mm}` (lesson index) is appended only when one division splits into multiple lesson plans, and is always present for natively two-level books (Class 1 English, Unit→পাঠ). **Re-amended D-PROJ00-059 (2026-05-31):** for natively two-level books the chapter address is `U{unit}_L{lesson}`, where **`L` denotes a book-hierarchy level only — never a teaching period** (this supersedes the brief 2026-05-29 reading that had used `_L{mm}` as the session index, which collided with English's lesson-level `L`). The **teaching period** uses a separate **`_S{period}`** slot that resets within the chapter — present on multi-period Session Plans, omitted on a single-period standalone — giving `C{class}_{SUBJECT}_{chapter-address}[_S{period}]_{Type}_v{ver}.{ext}`. Book-level artifacts (skeletons, stability reports/analyses) carry no `U`/`L`/`S`. See README §5.2 for the full pattern and worked examples.

### DRAFT

A prefix (`DRAFT_*`) or folder concept (`/drafts/`) marking work-in-progress files. Drafts may be edited freely. Compare with [LOCKED](#locked).

### Element-level Scan

The mandatory method (REF-21 §2) for building an annotated skeleton: the **textbook page is the curation surface**, so every element — title, each example word, each practice/decoding sentence, each exercise/counting item, each illustration — is read and tagged individually against the trigger lexicon, with a **separate illustration pass** and a per-division **scan ledger + completeness assertion** (spot-checking or inferring "formulaic" divisions is prohibited). Tagging from the stability skeleton, the Teacher Guide, or sample pages does not satisfy it. Adopted as master decision D-045 after the C1 Bangla skeleton pilot's theme-scan under-counted forced replacements. See [Illustration Pass](#illustration-pass), [Trigger Lexicon](#trigger-lexicon).

### Exam Anchor Set (EAS) (REF-26)

`LOCKED_REF-26_Exam_Anchor_Set_v1_0.md` (Project 00; LOCKED v1.0, 2026-07-17, D-PROJ00-072 / Project 04 D-PROJ04-015). The **data companion to the [Paper Assembly Template Standard](#paper-assembly-template-standard-pat-ref-25)**: every item on the 21 uploaded 2026 Sylhet final papers distilled into machine-checkable **anchors** — the specific facts (`F`), skills (`S`), word-lists (`W`), and ADMIN slots each paper tested, in 21 lane sections. A living data reference: each year's new finals replace their lane's anchors by supersede.

### Example Bank

A pre-prepared example inventory per teaching point in §2.2 Must-Cover Content of a lesson plan. Minimum 3 acceptable examples per teaching point per D-031. The teacher's fallback when they cannot or do not wish to invent their own example. New teachers default to picking from the bank; experienced teachers may substitute provided F-01 and the [Islamic Curation Policy](#islamic-curation-policy) hold. Format adapts per subject — vocabulary words for Bangla/English/Arabic; numerical examples for Math; observable phenomena for Science; ayat/hadith/adab scenarios for Islamic Studies; local places and civic scenarios for BGS; tools and procedures for ICT. (Subject Spine Playbooks REF-03+ specify per-subject form.) See REF-02 §4.4.

### Exit-Check Bank

A pre-prepared inventory of exit-check forms per objective type in a lesson plan's §2.4 Exit-Check, added at REF-02 v1.2 (2026-05-25) per D-043 / D-PROJ00-021. Minimum **2 ready forms per objective type** — the §2.4 counterpart of the [Example Bank](#example-bank), giving a teacher who cannot devise a check a ready form to run as a **per-student readout** (one shared check observed for each pupil, *not* one question per child). Format adapts per subject; the subject-specific exit-check forms live in the [Subject Spine Playbook](#subject-spine-playbook)s (REF-03 §3.6 for Bangla). See REF-02 §2.4 (rules 3, 6) and REF-03 §3.6.

### FLEXIBLE

Annotation on a lesson in the [Annotated Skeleton](#annotated-skeleton) marking it as acceptable either as-is or with modifications, at the teacher's discretion. Used when the content does not conflict with Islamic values but could be enhanced with Islamic framing.

### Flex Zone

Layer 3 of the [Three-Layer Lesson Plan](#three-layer-lesson-plan). The teacher's choice of examples (from the [Example Bank](#example-bank)), discovery patterns (from [Discovery Pattern Suggestions](#discovery-pattern-suggestions)), sequencing, pacing, and elaboration. Not reviewed for compliance against a script; reviewed only for whether learning outcomes were met. See REF-02 §4.

### Four-Layer Vocabulary Model

The REF-22 design that tags every word with exactly one layer: **Core** (must-own; replacement content reuses; tight stability-verified cap; tracked per-pupil in Project 06), **Working** (conversation/sentence-writing; +60–90 per class; spot-checked), **Receptive** (comprehension/story words; no cap; pre-taught at point of use; untracked), **Structure** (function words + fixed frames; mostly shared across classes; light spot-check). The layer names deliberately avoid "Tier" — that name is reserved for the *stability* axis (Universal / Stable / Drift / Singleton). Cross-cutting — reaches Projects 03 (lesson examples + homework pool), 04 (question-bank operative vocabulary), 05 (replacement-content required-vocabulary), 06 (per-pupil Core mastery). Locked at REF-22 v1.0 (D-PROJ00-031). See also: [Controlled Word-Bank](#controlled-word-bank-ref-22), [Vocabulary-draw rule](#vocabulary-draw-rule).

### Friday Review

Standing convention (PROJECT00_README.md §5.7): once a week, the Principal opens a chat per active Project and requests a weekly summary — what changed, what's in TODO, what's stuck, what's next. Estimated 30 minutes total across active Projects.

### Handoff Document

A markdown document produced at the end of any chat that generated meaningful work. Contents: accomplishments, decisions made, open items, output file locations, exact starting prompt for the next chat. Filename pattern: `PROJECT{NN}_handoff_YYYY-MM-DD_topic.md` (per D-011 and D-017). Saved in the source Project alongside other scaffolding files. The `PROJECT{NN}_` prefix lets handoffs from multiple Projects be downloaded into a single local folder without filename collisions.

### Homework Question Pool

The Bloom's-tagged question set per topic. **Lives in Project 04** — canonical production file → per-(class × subject) master → register (in Drive); ID `QP-{SUBJECT}-C{class}-U{nn}` (chapter scope; ≥20 per D-028, default 30/topic per Project 04 conventions 4-A). Each question Bloom's-tagged with answer key (or rubric for open-ended). **Not carried in lesson/session plans (master D-051, 2026-05-31, supersedes D-029)** — the former inline §2.8 Spine field is retired; a plan links to its questions only by the topic tag (`TOP-…`) the [Spine](#spine) declares. The teacher selects Y questions via the register/master per homework session (Y = the [Homework Specification](#homework-specification) guideline count; D-030 unchanged). Under **REF-08 ([Homework Architecture](#homework-architecture)) §4** the ≥20 minimum sustains routine rotation **and** bounded reactive top-ups for weak students (selected from the same topic's Pool, never authored). See Project 04 conventions 4-A, REF-08 §4, and REF-02 (§2.8 retired).

### Homework Specification

The 7th conceptual field of the [Spine](#spine), added at REF-02 v1.1 (2026-05-20). Declares what homework the lesson assigns: time budget per session (Class-1 band **0–40 min, default 20**, per REF-08 §2.2 — the per-subject lever in the floating-allocation method), guideline question count (Class 1 pilot 5–10 from the [Homework Question Pool](#homework-question-pool)), Bloom's distribution of homework (Remember/Understand-heavy at primary classes since homework lacks teacher scaffolding), and traceability rule (each homework item ties to §2.2 Must-Cover Content or §2.6 [Revision Anchor](#revision-anchor) item). Each subject's §2.7 time is set per-lesson by that day's topic load, then summed and reconciled against the [Daily Homework Budget](#daily-homework-budget) ceiling by the class teacher (REF-08 §2.3). Time is the firm cap; question count is the lever (D-030). Zero homework permitted (some lessons may not warrant homework). See REF-02 §2.7 and REF-08 §2 / §4.

### Illustration Pass

The separate image-only sweep within an element-level scan (REF-21 §2 step 3): an illustration can trigger a curation category (C-02/C-03/C-04/C-05/C-18) even when the surrounding text is clean — instruments drawn in a scene, hero portraits, dolls/figurines, festival decorations, or a face used to teach a body part. Run after the text pass so image triggers are not missed. See [Element-level Scan](#element-level-scan).

### Islamic Curation Policy

The canonical written policy governing when NCTB content is replaced, what categories trigger replacement (idolatry, mixed-gender depictions, music/dance, festivals of other faiths, problematic depictions of living beings, gambling, alcohol, riba, secular framings contradicting tawhid), what must be preserved when replacing (learning outcome, vocabulary, Bloom's level), and edge cases. Drafted and iterated in Project 02; locked v1.0+ copy canonical in Project 00.

### KEEP-AS-IS

Annotation on a lesson in the [Annotated Skeleton](#annotated-skeleton) marking it as acceptable without modification under the Islamic Curation Policy.

### Lean Project Scaffolding Standard (REF-23)

The canonical reference (LOCKED v1.0, 2026-05-28, D-PROJ00-035) defining the **minimum scaffolding** a Project must carry and the stepwise procedure for standing one up. Its purpose is token discipline: a bound Project **inherits** the master §5 conventions by a single pointer line (master §5.5/§5.12/§5.13/§5.14 per D-047/D-048) rather than restating them, keeping local files lean. **Scope = all-future** — it binds every Project scaffolded on or after 2026-05-28 (Projects 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, and any later Project) via a forward-looking clause; **Projects 01–03 are exempt** (already scaffolded) and **Project 00 is the source hub** (holds the conventions, does not inherit). ### Salafi Methodology

The default theological framing of the school's curriculum (per D-020): adherence to the Quran, the authentic Sunnah, and the understanding of the Salaf al-Salih — the first three generations of Muslims (the Sahaba, the Tabi'un, and the Atba al-Tabi'in). Rulings derive from direct evidence understood per the Salaf's example. The policy does not bind to the four traditional madhhabs (taqlid) as the default mode.

The operational expression is the [Islamic Curation Policy](#islamic-curation-policy) — what gets replaced in NCTB content reflects this framing. When NCTB content reflects general non-Salafi Bangladeshi Islamic culture (mawlid/milad celebrations, urs and saint veneration, grave-related practices, music in Islamic context, certain Sufi-literary religious framings), it is flagged for replacement. Expected replacement workload is higher under this framing than under a Hanafi-default framing.

Scholarship favored in this framing: Quran tafsir from Salaf-aligned scholars (Ibn Kathir, At-Tabari, As-Sa'di), authenticated hadith (Bukhari, Muslim, Sunan collections, Al-Albani's gradings), classical scholars (Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, Muhammad ibn Abdul Wahhab), contemporary scholars (Bin Baz, Al-Albani, Ibn Uthaymeen, Salih al-Fawzan).

### Session Map

A field of the [Chapter Plan](#chapter-plan), REF-02 **§2.9** (new in v1.3). One row per session within the chapter — columns: session # · focus · session objective · must-cover slice (from §2.2) · exit-check (from the §2.4 Bank) · homework slice (topic + count, from the Project 04 register). The Session Map is the **bridge** between the chapter-scope [Spine](#spine) and the period-scope [Session Plans](#session-plan): each Session Plan **copies** its objective and slices from its Session Map row (REF-02 §2A.1 — *reference, never restate*). Adding or removing a row after Session Plans exist is a Chapter-Plan revision, not an in-place edit. A single-period chapter has **no** Session Map.

A single standalone, living file tracking how each topic in a subject develops across classes, to inform per-year emphasis. One file for all stability-scope subjects (BAN/ENG/MATH/SCI/BGS), each a self-contained section with a readiness banner, populated **incrementally** as a subject's class-stack of [stability](#stability-analysis) skeletons ([REF-05](#stability-analysis)) completes. Uses a **hybrid controlled vocabulary** (canonical Topic IDs; the first mapped subject seeds the shared category headers, subjects otherwise self-seed bottom-up); records each topic's per-class **rung** (native label + source + depth) and **prerequisite edges**, from which **[Load-bearing](#load-bearing-topic) scoring** and prerequisite/coverage **gap detection** derive. **Append-only class rungs** absorb a new class each year without restructuring. Governed by the **"deeper-not-faster" guardrail**: the map informs depth and foundation-laying, never acceleration into next year's [Spine](#spine). A **parallel downstream aggregation layer** on the D-005 build order — it consumes completed skeletons and feeds the [Subject Spine Playbooks](#subject-spine-playbook) (REF-03) and, through them, lesson-plan emphasis decisions; it does not re-order the build chain. **Subjects mapped: Bangla** (Classes 1–5, fully TG-reconciled — REF-19 v1.1–v1.4), **English** (Classes 1–5, fully TG-reconciled — REF-19 v1.5, 2026-05-27, D-PROJ00-026; reuses Bangla's shared literacy headers, self-seeds its own boxes), **and Math** (Classes 1–5, fully TG-reconciled — REF-19 v1.6, 2026-05-27, D-PROJ00-028; the **first non-literacy subject** — self-seeds seven math-native [category headers](#category-header-ref-19), so REF-19 now carries two header families; 24 topics; 8 High / 8 Medium / 8 Low; 2 🟦 PROTECTED both free-thinking). Science/BGS still stubbed. See REF-19 and README §6.

### Vocabulary-draw rule

The per-artifact rule (REF-22 §0.2 / D-PROJ00-031) for which [Four-Layer Vocabulary Model](#four-layer-vocabulary-model) layers each downstream artifact may draw on, ensuring vocabulary is used only at the right point in a pupil's learning: **lesson-plan examples** = Core + Working + Structure (+ a lesson's target word or a Receptive word pre-taught in the same lesson); **homework** = Core + Working + only-words-taught; **question bank** = operative vocabulary owned (Core + Working + already-taught), with Receptive permitted only inside a pre-taught or glossed comprehension passage; **Project-05 replacement content** preserves Core + Working when text is rewritten under REF-01. Loaded by Projects 03/04/05/06 when consuming REF-22.
