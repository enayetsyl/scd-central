## What blocks on this lane

Nothing merges into the book on a reviewer flag alone — the validator governs merge. But
**nothing is promoted to `gold` in the Hub while a row here is OPEN** (AGENTS.md §11: teacher
review then Principal promotion; this lane sits alongside).

## How a row closes

The reviewer records the ruling in the row, and any content change it causes goes through the
normal loop — patch → validator → merge. A row is never closed by editing the book directly.
