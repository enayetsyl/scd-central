## §1 — Science subject rules (REF-03 C3 slice)

### 1.1 The four load-bearing spines — C3 lays the *bottom* rungs (rule S-2)
Science is a **branching web of inquiry, not a ladder of recall** (REF-03 §0). Four strands carry the structural load (🔴 High); the rest are shallow 1–2-rung branches. **C3 is the origins year — lay the bottom rung; never pre-teach the next class's rung.** The C3 rungs:

| Spine | C3 rung (this class) | Leads to | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Force ladder** (`SCI-FORCE`, F) | **force changes shape & volume** (U06, ৪.১) — **NCF-new origin** | C4 motion → C5 friction/gravity/machines | the map's cleanest ladder; POE-native; teach as first-principles |
| **Astro ladder** (`SCI-ASTRO`, S) | **the Sun** as light/heat/energy source (U09, ৬.১) — **NCF-new origin** | C4 solar system → C5 Earth's motion | **tawhid-safe framing mandatory** (§2 rule 3); scientific only |
| **Matter substrate** (`SCI-MATTER`, P) | **macroscopic states** incl. air-as-matter + **magnetism** (U04, ৩.১/৩.২) | C4 properties/change → C5 particle level | confirm the macroscopic picture firmly — it is the base of the C5 particle step |
| **Plant / living substrate** (`SCI-PLANT`/`SCI-ANIMAL`, L) | **external structure & organ function** (U01 ১.১; U02 ১.২) + **classify/care** (১.৩) | C4 growth → C5 ecology | observation-led; C4 growth is new ground, not a re-run of C3 structure |

> **Rule S-1 — `strand ≠ chapter`.** Read the যোগ্যতা off the chapter header, never the chapter number (§5 rows are TG-validated). **Rule S-1b — পাঠ numbers restart per chapter at C3** (sum 69); never cross-read with C4/C5. Watch the chapter-opener পাঠ-count line, which can misprint — cross-check against the contiguous পাঠ chain.

### 1.5 Science Example Bank conventions (REF-02 §4.4; ≥3 per teaching point)

### 1.6 Science Discovery-Pattern library (REF-02 §4.5; ≥3 per chapter; pick from these)
Science's native inquiry pedagogy is the gift — these are NCF-native, not a school graft:
1. **POE — অনুমান → পর্যবেক্ষণ → ব্যাখ্যা** (the flagship; Hook + investigation) · 2. **Real-object observation (বাস্তব বস্তু পর্যবেক্ষণ)** — observe before naming (rule S-3) · 3. **Sort-and-justify (শ্রেণিবদ্ধ করো ও কারণ বলো)** — the `SCI-CLASSIFY` vehicle, strong at C3 (U01/U02) · 4. **Demonstration-led (পরীক্ষণ/ডেমো)** — safe demo; record what changed (force, magnet, state) · 5. **Question-led (কেন / কী হলে কী হতো)** — open question before content (একাকী চিন্তা) · 6. **Argue–counter-argue (যুক্তি-পাল্টাযুক্তি)** — paired/small-group claim vs counter (same-gender per C-02) · 7. **Investigate-and-measure (পরিমাপ করে দেখি)** — links to Math measurement (U09 shadow length) · 8. **Design-your-own-solution / sequence-your-own (নিজের সমাধান বানাই)** — `SCI-ICT`, **PROTECTED, keep open** (U11) · 9. **Nature-walk / collection (প্রকৃতি থেকে সংগ্রহ)** — homework-friendly (U01/U02 plants & animals) · 10. *(Systems-trace is the C5 `SCI-ECOLOGY` capstone — not used at C3.)*

## §2 — Curation crosswalk (C3 Science) + standing rules

Almost all of it is **image-level** (C-05 faces, C-02 mixed-gender child characters), handled by **SR1/REF-24**; content triggers are rare and small. Highest-yield categories at C3 Science, in scan-frequency order (AnnSkel §3):
