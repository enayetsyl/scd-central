## §1 — BGS subject rules (REF-03 C3 slice)

### 1.1 The four ladders + C3 rungs — honour the through-line (deeper-not-faster)
BGS is structurally **flat — only one High-load topic** (most lessons are *terminal*, taught for in-year mastery, not as a prerequisite). The four exceptions the author must honour at C3:

- **`BGS-COUNTRY` substrate 🔴 High** — location & 64 districts (**U09, ৭.১**) **opens** the spine (districts C3 → landforms/rivers C4 → maps C5). It feeds the five "of-Bangladesh" topics; secure C3 district mastery before the C4 step.
- **Rights ladder (R) 🟠** — family & school role (**U06, ৫.১+৫.২**) → child rights & road safety (**U07, ৫.৪+৫.৫**). Rights vocabulary spirals family → society → state (C4 citizen rights → C5 state/society). Teach the C3 rungs as foundations, not throwaways.
- **Money ladder (F) 🟢** — money use & need-vs-want (**U11, ৯.১**) **opens** the use → conserve → budget spiral (C4 conserve → C5 budget/bank). **No riba at C3** (clean baseline; frugality / no-israf is an alignment asset, §4).
- **Depth-spirals (same code, deepening framing):** ENV → environment (**U01, ১.১**, deepens to C5 climate); ETHICS → interpersonal akhlaq (**U08, ৬.১**, the akhlaq anchor, deepens to C5 tolerance); GEOGRAPHY world→continent (**U05, ৪.১**, a "narrowing zoom" to C4 Asia → C5 South Asia).

> **Rule BGS-1 (strand ≠ chapter; apply the −1 offset).** Read the যোগ্যতা code from the §5 row, never from the chapter number — and remember the 2026 অধ্যায় anchor runs −1 to the TG chapter for Ch4 onward (already resolved in §5).
> **Rule BGS-2 (strand-presence facts at C3).** **Professions (strand 8) IS present at C3** (U10, ৮.১) — it is terminal and **drops at C5** (the only intra-subject strand drop; track forward, do not expect it at C5). The **leaders chapter is dropped at C3** (the 2026 reprint removed it — the cleanest C3 political trajectory of the reprint run) and is **reintroduced standalone only at C5**.

### 1.2 Required terms / place-names / dates (C3 slice; Must-Cover part C)
BGS is term-and-place heavy; place-name and date accuracy is **non-negotiable**. The required list for any lesson = its TG থিম vocabulary + the topic's canonical place-names / civic terms. C3 anchors by strand:
- **Geography (U05, ৪.১):** সাত মহাদেশ (এশিয়া · ইউরোপ · আফ্রিকা · উত্তর আমেরিকা · দক্ষিণ আমেরিকা · ওশেনিয়া · অ্যান্টার্কটিকা) · পাঁচ মহাসাগর · ভূগোলক / মানচিত্র · বাংলাদেশের অবস্থান · পার্শ্ববর্তী দেশ (ভারত · মিয়ানমার).
- **Country (U09, ৭.১+৭.২):** ৬৪ জেলা · ৮ বিভাগ · সীমানা · কৃষিজাত পণ্য · শিল্পজাত পণ্য (the classify pair).
- **Rights / safety (U06/U07):** পরিবার · বিদ্যালয় · মৌলিক অধিকার · শিশু সুরক্ষা · ট্রাফিক চিহ্ন · সড়ক-নিরাপত্তা · জরুরি সংস্থা.
- **Disaster (U12, ১০.১):** অগ্নিকান্ড · বন্যা · ভূমিকম্প · জরুরি ফোন **৯৯৯ / ১০৬** · দুর্যোগ-প্রস্তুতি.
- **Ethics (U08, ৬.১):** ন্যায় ও অন্যায় · দয়া · সততা · পরোপকার · সহযোগিতা · ক্ষমা · সহনশীলতা.

### 1.3 Bloom band (C3) — read the band off the Core; apply the knowledge-vs-judgement split
REF-03 does **not** reprint the numeric band (avoids drift) — read the **C3 band off the Core's Bloom pocket (REF-18 V1C / REF-06 §3.6)**. The BGS rule that *does* live here: **set §2.3 from which kind of outcome the শিখনফল names, not from "it's a social-studies chapter."**

### 1.4 The inquiry cycle (the distinctive BGS requirement — protect the individual-then-group order)
The NCF inquiry cycle **পর্যবেক্ষণ → কাজ-ক (individual) → কাজ-খ (group/জোড়ায়) → আলোচনা → মূল্যায়ন** *is* the school's free-thinking / self-try posture and is the **default discovery backbone** for every C3 BGS chapter. **Protect the individual step** (কাজ-ক) — where independent thinking happens — and the **own-opinion / judgement** outcomes (ethics/rights/environment). Do not collapse the cycle into teacher-talk. Pair/group work is **same-gender** (SR-2).

### 1.5 Example Bank conventions (REF-02 §4.4; ≥3 per teaching point)
**Form for BGS = concrete instances** (a continent, a district, a product, an occupation, a moral scenario, a place-name, a disaster-response step). Drawn from the §1.2 required terms where possible (so examples double as required-term practice) and **localised to the school's region (Sylhet)** where the strand allows (e.g. the local district, a nearby river, a local occupation). All examples **F-01- and curation-clean.** Islamic-resonant examples welcome `[Islamic-add]`: returning a found item / amanah and honouring parents (ethics, U08); the dignity of halal labour (professions, U10); environment/resources as **Allah's ni'mah and amanah** (stewardship, U01); frugality / no-israf and halal saving (money, U11). For any living-being / people example, prefer the **term or scenario, not a picture** (C-05).

### 1.6 Discovery-pattern library (REF-02 §4.5; ≥3 per lesson; pick from these)
1. **Inquiry cycle (NCF default)** — পর্যবেক্ষণ → কাজ-ক → কাজ-খ → আলোচনা → মূল্যায়ন (protect individual-then-group). · 2. **Map-led / locate-it** (U05 continents/oceans, U09 districts). · 3. **Sorting / classify-led** (কৃষিজাত vs শিল্পজাত products U09; professions U10; rights-vs-duties cards U07). · 4. **Scenario / case-led** — moral/rights scenario; judge and justify (the Evaluate reach — U08/U07). · 5. **Question-led (কী / কেন / কীভাবে)** — open question before content; hypothesise. · 6. **Paired/group exploration (জোড়ায় / দলগত — same-gender, SR-2).** · 7. **Timeline / sequence-led** — a money/budget sequence (U11); a disaster-response sequence (U12). · 8. **Do-it / practise as oneself** — a disaster-response drill (U12), a road-safety crossing (U07), a budget calculation **as oneself** (the C-19 boundary — never impersonate a character). · 9. **Own-opinion prompt (নিজের মতামত / যুক্তি দেখাও)** — the free-thinking flagship for BGS (U08 ethics). · 10. **Local-investigation (অনুসন্ধান)** — gather a fact from home/community (a local occupation, a family saving habit, the local district) and report.
