## How to use this log

Append-only chronological record of Project-03-internal decisions: architecture decisions, operational decisions, and the absorption records when Project 03 adopts an initiative-wide convention or curation flag. Each row carries an ID (`D-PROJ03-NNN`), date, the decision, its reason/source, and a status. To overturn a decision, add a new superseding row (see the protocol below) — never edit an existing row.

## Relationship to the master decision log

Initiative-wide decisions that affect multiple Projects (**D-001 through D-051** and successors) live in **`PROJECT00_README.md` §3** and are *not* duplicated here. This log carries Project-03-internal decisions only. Where Project 03 adopts a master convention/flag, the adoption is recorded here and the corresponding master propagation flag is acknowledged in the [propagation-flags section](#cross-project-propagation-flags-pending-principal-action).

## Reopen / supersede protocol

1. **Never edit an existing row.** The log is append-only.
2. To overturn a decision, add a new row referencing the one it replaces: the Decision cell starts `SUPERSEDES D-PROJ03-NNN:`; the Reason cell explains what changed and why now; the old row's Status changes to `Superseded by D-PROJ03-MMM`.
3. If a reversal would also affect `PROJECT00_README.md` §3 or another Project's DECISIONS.md, flag it via the propagation-flags section **before** logging.
4. If unsure whether a decision belongs here or in the master README, default to flagging it for the master README.
5. **Housekeeping decisions** (convention absorptions, version bumps of consumed references, etc.) are still logged as new rows, since they change Project 03's own state.
